## Three rounds without closing — worth another round?

**No. Scope down.** The pattern across R2, R3 and R4 is not "nearly there" — it is a stable failure mode: each round fixes the *reported instance* and leaves or relocates the *vector*, and this round did it three times (N1 relocated word-order dependence into inversion, N10 relocated bracket inflation from pages to `[registry]` at identical magnitude, N3 relocated a false exclusion from absence-probing to term-kind-probing). Twice the round also shipped a test that pins the surviving hole open (`test_verify.py:1054`, `test_eval_answers.py:1076-1093`, `test_adjudicate_claims.py:272-274`). A fourth round on the same surface will most likely produce a fourth relocation.

Concretely:

**Revert, do not re-fix.** Roll `verify.py` back to head for all three regressions — the `_field_context_amounts` rewrite, the `_check_existence` polarity flip, and the `:1713` promotion call. Head *misses* conflicts (false negatives); work *inverts* them (false positives on true prose, plus the false negatives). Missing is the safe failure and it is what the corpus has today. This also deletes findings #1, #3, #7, #10 and #17 in one commit and restores the anchor to a baseline nobody has to argue about.

**Keep and commit the parts that are actually done** on a narrow branch: N11, N12, N13, fix 22's cost reduction (documented in code as a permanently-zero tripwire), `_carry_cleared`, and the N14 evidence half. These are independently verified, live-safe, and blocked by nothing.

**Land two one-line metric fixes** with blanket invariant tests: `eval_answers.py:791` notes-only exclusion, and `:1324/:1340/:1377` aggregation of `citation_complete_notes_only`.

**Split the adjudication instrument onto its own branch.** N3/N4/N5/N15/N16/N20 and fix 38 do not depend on verifier calibration at all; coupling them to `verify.py` is what produced the stale-row problem and forces the sidecar to be rebuilt on someone else's schedule.

**Then re-scope the verifier work** as a single design pass on `_scoped_field_conflict` and the field-window assignment together, with the pin written *before* the fix and parametrized over the dimension the code measures (connector/punctuation and cited scope), not the dimension the last bug report happened to exhibit. Do not reopen `VERIFY_REPAIR` until that pass has its own fail-before evidence.

Anchor and metric restatement (N21) must wait for the revert — 83/146 is not a defensible baseline while it contains two never-checked sentences and a contract that does not match its published name.
