# SSA CHATBOT

### GCF Proposal Knowledge Assistant

Ask questions across the indexed Green Climate Fund proposal collection and receive answers grounded in retrieved document evidence.

- Explore individual funding proposals.
- Compare projects, financing, risks, safeguards, and expected results.
- Follow the cited sources to verify important claims.

SSA CHATBOT is provided by **Sustainable Solutions for Africa**. It supports research and document review; always verify high-stakes decisions against the cited source documents.
