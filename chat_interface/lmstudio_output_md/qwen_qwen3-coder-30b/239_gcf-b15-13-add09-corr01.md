# GCF/B.15/13/Add.09/Corr.01  
## Page 1  

1. In relation to the funding proposal FP036, the accredited entity has informed the Secretariat that the executing entity for the subproject 2 –SIDS Program Support Technical Assistance- will be the Asian Development Bank, which will provide the relevant technical assistance to the target countries. Therefore, all the references to “implementing agency” and “executing agency” for the subproject 2 -SIDS Program Support Technical Assistance- shall be read as executing entity and shall refer to the Asian Development Bank.  

2. Furthermore, for the abovementioned subproject 2 -SIDS Program Support Technical Assistance-, the Asian Development Bank, as executing entity of this subproject, will enter into letter agreements with each of the target countries to which the SIDS Program Support Technical Assistance will be provided. Therefore, all the references to “memorandum of understanding” or “MOU” with respect to this subproject 2 shall be read as letter agreements.  

3. For the implementation of the subproject 1 - Cook Islands subproject-, the accredited entity has informed the Secretariat that the executing entities will be Te Aponga Uira and Cook Islands. Therefore, all the references to “implementing agency” and “executing agency” for subproject 1- Cook Islands subproject- shall be read as executing entities and shall refer to both Te Aponga Uira and Cook Islands.
