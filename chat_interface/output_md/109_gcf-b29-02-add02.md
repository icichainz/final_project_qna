# Green Climate Fund Funding Proposal V.2.1

## Supervision and Verification

CABEI will contract a third-party verification company, paid by the borrower (0.4% of the loan amount), with field presence. The verifier will be present during project execution to verify design products, the global budget, and evaluate variations between real and programmed physical and financial execution. They will also analyze the effect on contractual completion time, conduct technical audits, and issue monthly reports to CABEI.

CABEI's supervision will be conducted by the Credit Operations Supervision Area (USOC) through reports from the verifying firm and field visits, according to the annual supervision plan. At least two supervision visits per year will be made. Environmental and social aspects will be monitored by the Bank's Office of Environmental and Social Sustainability (OFAS).

# Secretariat’s Assessment of FP166

## Proposal Name: Light Rail Transit for the Greater Metropolitan Area (GAM)

- **Accredited Entity:** Central American Bank for Economic Integration (CABEI)
- **Country/(ies):** Costa Rica
- **Project/Programme Size:** Large

## I. Overall Assessment of the Secretariat

1. **Strengths:**
   - High greenhouse gas (GHG) impact due to electrification powered by > 98% renewable sources.
   - Key role in systematic change in public transport, integrated with other transport modes.
   - High financing leverage from the concessionaire and private sector.

2. **Points of Caution:**
   - Continuous fiscal burden expected by the government for annual subsidy payment.
   - Selection of concessionaire critical due to high upfront capital investment.

## II. Summary of the Secretariat’s Assessment

### 2.1 Project Background

- **Objective:** Establish a modern, fast, safe, and environmentally sound electric light rail transit (LRT) system with 85 km of double tracks on five lines.
- **Impact:** Transport 63 million passengers annually, benefit 2.7 million people, and reduce GHG emissions by 7.62 million tCO2eq over 44 years.
- **Total Project Financing:** USD 1.8 billion, with GCF providing USD 250 million in loans and USD 21.3 million in grants.

### 2.2 Component-by-Component Analysis

#### Component 1: LRT System

- **Total Cost:** USD 1.852 billion; **GCF Cost:** USD 250 million
- **Infrastructure and Systems:** USD 834 million
- **Rolling Stock:** USD 452 million for 78 trains
- **Financing Charges:** USD 298 million
- **Other Costs:** USD 268 million including land acquisition and environmental measures

#### Component 2: Urban Integration with NMT and Connectivity/Accessibility

- **Total Cost:** USD 20 million; **GCF Cost:** USD 20 million
- **Features:** 16 km of cycle lanes, infrastructure for connectivity/accessibility at six stations

#### Component 3: Capacity-Building and Gender Measures

- **Total Cost:** USD 1.3 million; **GCF Cost:** USD 1.3 million
