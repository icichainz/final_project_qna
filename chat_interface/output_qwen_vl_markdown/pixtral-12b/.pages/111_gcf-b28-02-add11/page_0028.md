## Activity 1.4:

### Exits:

At the core of the GGEF’s business model is to drive sustainable growth of portfolio companies to further exit upon investment maturity (typically investments are held for 4 to 6 years once investment in the portfolio company has been committed). GGEF’s exit strategy is based on selling shares to another investor (“follow-on” investor) through sale to strategic buyers, sale to financial buyers, initial public offering, or management team of the investee company “management buyout”. GGEF seeks to create sustained, positive impact through their investments, even after exit. This is achieved by building sustainable business models with a positive impact mandate and seeking out potential buyers with commitment to continue such a mandate.
