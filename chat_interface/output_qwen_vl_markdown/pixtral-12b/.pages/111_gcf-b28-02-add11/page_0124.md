# GENDER ASSESSMENT AND GENDER ACTION PLAN FOR GREEN GROWTH EQUITY FUND

## Final Report

### GENDER ACTION PLAN

- Reporting on workforce related data disaggregated by gender to be determined at each portfolio level during Gender Assessment, but minimum target is once per year.
  - Specific Gender Action Plan, but at minimum annual reporting.

- Incorporate Gender Inclusion during stakeholder consultations at project locations and during project impact assessment
  - Require stakeholder engagement activities at project level through Project information meetings, ESIA consultations, etc., with records of attendees maintained by gender. Set minimum quorum requirements by gender to ensure that SEP reports voices risks and benefits of men, women, and vulnerable groups.
  - Report including records of attendees maintained by gender on Stakeholder Engagement activities by the Portfolio Companies submitted to GGEF for investment due-diligence or post-investment monitoring.

- Timeline to be determined in project specific Gender Action Plan, but at minimum annual reporting.

#### Portfolio Companies
- Fund Manager investment team to undertake monitoring based on disclosed documents.
- Supervision and support by GGEF Gender Focal Point.
- Drive preparation of project level Stakeholder Engagement Plan (SEP) and Grievance Redressal Mechanism (GRM), with women as a separate stakeholder category. SEPs to ensure that consultations are culturally appropriate and conducted in gender responsive manner, with vulnerable groups also represented. This information will be required for Environmental and Social Assessment and Gender Assessment by GGEF.
  - Evidence of inclusion of requirement to perform gender-responsive Stakeholder Engagement in Gender Action Plan to be developed for Portfolio Companies. Quantitative indicators (such as % of minimum woman participation required, number of meetings held exclusively for women) to be determined in individual project level SEP given the local context of the project. During SE, if woman participation is limited, encourage portfolio companies to perform outreach events to increase woman participation.

- Timeline to be determined in project specific Gender Action Plan.
