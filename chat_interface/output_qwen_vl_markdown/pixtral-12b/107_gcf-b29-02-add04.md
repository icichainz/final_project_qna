About the vulnerability presented by remote communities:
Remote communities are particularly vulnerable to the impacts of climate change due to their reliance on natural resources for livelihoods and subsistence. These communities often lack access to reliable electricity, clean water, and sanitation infrastructure. Implementing DRE technologies in these areas can significantly improve living conditions, enhance economic opportunities, and support sustainable development.
