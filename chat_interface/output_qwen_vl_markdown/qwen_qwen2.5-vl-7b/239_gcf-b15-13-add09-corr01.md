# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum IX - Corrigendum

## Funding proposal package for FP036

### Summary

This document presents the relevant amendments to the funding proposal FP036 – "Pacific Islands Renewable Energy Investment Program" contained in the document GCF/B.15/2014/Corr.09, "Consideration of funding proposals Addendum IX," to be considered by the Board of the Green Climate Fund at its fifteenth meeting.

**Page 2**
---

1. In relation to the funding proposal FP036, the accredited entity has informed the Secretariat that the executing entity for the subproject 2 - SIDS Program Support Technical Assistance and Subregional Development Strategy, which is a grant for technical assistance to the target countries. Therefore, all the references to "implementing agency" and "executing agency" in the letter agreements with each of the target countries to which the SIDS Program Support Technical Assistance and Subregional Development Strategy is directed shall be read as executing entity and shall refer to the Asian Development Bank.

2. Furthermore, for the abovementioned subproject 2 - SIDS Program Support Technical Assistance and Subregional Development Strategy, the accredited entity has entered into letter agreements with each of the target countries to which the SIDS Program Support Technical Assistance and Subregional Development Strategy is directed. Therefore, all references to "understanding" or "MOP" with respect to this subproject 2 shall be read as letter agreements.

3. For the implementation of the subproject 1 - Cook Islands subproject, the accredited entity has entered into a letter agreement with both Te Aponga Uira and the Government of Cook Islands. Therefore, all the references to "implementing agency" and "executing agency" for subproject 1 - Cook Islands subproject shall be read as executing entities and shall refer to both Te Aponga Uira and Cook Islands.
