# Funding Proposal

## Project Title: Renewable Barbados Project

## Country(ies): Barbados

## Accredited Entity: International Finance Corporation

## Date of first submission: 2023-09-18

## Date of current submission: 2023-09-27

## Version number: V.010

## Funding Proposal

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is pleased to announce the funding proposal for the Renewable Barbados Project. This project aims to support the transition of Barbados towards a low-carbon economy by promoting renewable energy sources and energy efficiency measures.

## Project Overview

The Renewable Barbados Project will focus on the following key areas:

1. **Renewable Energy Generation**: The project will explore the potential for generating electricity from renewable sources such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power in Barbados. This will help to reduce the country's reliance on fossil fuels and lower greenhouse gas emissions.

2. **Energy Efficiency Measures**: The project will also focus on implementing energy efficiency measures in various sectors of the economy, including buildings, transportation, and industry. This will help to reduce energy consumption and further contribute to the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions.

3. **Capacity Building**: The project will provide training and capacity building programs for local stakeholders, including government officials, businesses, and community members. This will help to ensure that Barbados has the necessary skills and knowledge to implement and maintain renewable energy projects.

## Project Budget

The total budget for the Renewable Barbados Project is $50 million over a period of five years (2023-2027). The budget will be allocated as follows:

1. **Renewable Energy Generation**: $10 million
2. **Energy Efficiency Measures**: $10 million
3. **Capacity Building**: $10 million
4. **Project Management and Administration**: $5 million

## Project Timeline

The Renewable Barbados Project is expected to be completed by 2027, with the following milestones:

1. **Phase 1 (2023-2024)**: Conduct a feasibility study and develop a detailed project plan.
2. **Phase 2 (2025-2026)**: Implement renewable energy generation projects and energy efficiency measures in selected sectors of the economy.
3. **Phase 3 (2027)**: Conduct capacity building programs for local stakeholders and evaluate the project's impact on greenhouse gas emissions and energy consumption.

## Conclusion

The Renewable Barbados Project is a critical step towards Barbados' transition to a low-carbon economy. By investing in renewable energy generation, energy efficiency measures, and capacity building programs, the project will help to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, improve energy security, and create new opportunities for economic growth and development.

The Green Climate Fund is committed to supporting Barbados in its efforts to achieve these goals and is pleased to announce this funding proposal for the Renewable Barbados Project.

## Introduction

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is a global initiative that supports projects and programs that reduce greenhouse gas emissions and enhance climate resilience. This report focuses on the gender assessment of renewable energy projects in Barbados, specifically the Renewable Barbados (RSB) program.

## Background

Barbados is a small island nation located in the Caribbean Sea. The country has been committed to reducing its greenhouse gas emissions and transitioning to renewable energy sources. The GCF's RSB program aims to support projects that promote sustainable development, enhance climate resilience, and reduce poverty.

## Gender Assessment

The gender assessment of the RSB program in Barbados was conducted by the Green Climate Fund (GCF) and the Barbados Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources. The assessment focused on the following areas:

1. **Project Selection**: The assessment evaluated the selection process for renewable energy projects, including the representation of women in project teams and decision-making bodies.
2. **Project Implementation**: The assessment assessed the implementation of renewable energy projects, including the participation of women in project activities and the impact of these projects on gender equality.
3. **Policy and Regulation**: The assessment evaluated the policy and regulatory framework for renewable energy projects, including the representation of women in policy-making bodies and the impact of these policies on gender equality.
4. **Capacity Building**: The assessment assessed the capacity building programs for women in the field of renewable energy, including training and education opportunities.
5. **Monitoring and Evaluation**: The assessment evaluated the monitoring and evaluation systems for renewable energy projects, including the representation of women in monitoring and evaluation bodies.

## Findings

The gender assessment of the RSB program in Barbados found that:

1. Women are underrepresented in project teams and decision-making bodies.
2. Women are less likely to participate in renewable energy projects than men.
3. Women face barriers to accessing finance for renewable energy projects, including limited access to credit and collateral.
4. There is a lack of capacity building programs for women in the field of renewable energy.
5. The monitoring and evaluation systems for renewable energy projects are not inclusive of women.

## Recommendations

The Green Climate Fund recommends that Barbados take the following actions to address gender inequality in renewable energy:

1. Increase the representation of women in project teams and decision-making bodies.
2. Provide training and education opportunities for women in the field of renewable energy.
3. Develop financing mechanisms specifically for women-led renewable energy projects.
4. Establish monitoring and evaluation systems that include women as a key stakeholder.
5. Promote gender equality in all aspects of renewable energy policy and planning.
