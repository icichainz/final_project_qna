# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum V

## Funding proposal package for FP042

### Summary

This addendum contains the following three parts:

1. **A funding proposal titled "Irrigation development and adaptation of irrigated agriculture to climate change in semi-arid Morocco" submitted by AFD.**
2. **An eco-objective project proposal titled "Ecological restoration and social rehabilitation (EIRS) in the Korean Peninsula."**
3. **A funding proposal package for FP042, which includes the above two proposals.**

The documents are presented as submitted by the accredited entity, and national designated authority or focal point, respectively.
