## APPPRAISAL SUMMARY

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is a global initiative that aims to mobilize $100 billion annually by 2020 to support climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts in developing countries. This proposal outlines the GCF's funding priorities for the year 2021, focusing on projects that address the most pressing climate-related challenges.

## Funding Priorities for 2021

### Agriculture and Rural Development

The GCF will prioritize projects that enhance agricultural productivity, improve rural livelihoods, and reduce greenhouse gas emissions from the agricultural sector. This includes:

- **Climate-Smart Agriculture**: Projects that promote sustainable farming practices, such as agroforestry, conservation agriculture, and integrated pest management, to increase resilience to climate change impacts.
- **Rural Livelihoods**: Initiatives that support smallholder farmers in developing countries by providing access to markets, credit, and insurance, enabling them to adapt to climate change while maintaining their livelihoods.

### Energy and Climate Change Mitigation

The GCF will focus on projects that reduce greenhouse gas emissions from the energy sector and enhance energy security in developing countries. This includes:

- **Renewable Energy**: Projects that promote the development of renewable energy sources, such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power, to reduce dependence on fossil fuels and mitigate climate change impacts.
- **Energy Efficiency**: Initiatives that improve energy efficiency in buildings, transportation, and industry, reducing energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.

### Water and Sanitation

The GCF will prioritize projects that enhance water security and sanitation in developing countries, particularly in the context of climate change. This includes:

- **Water Management**: Projects that improve water management practices, such as irrigation systems, water harvesting, and wastewater treatment, to ensure sustainable water use and reduce water scarcity.
- **Sanitation**: Initiatives that promote access to safe sanitation facilities, including toilets and waste disposal systems, to protect public health and prevent the spread of diseases.

### Climate Change Adaptation and Resilience

The GCF will focus on projects that enhance the resilience of communities to climate change impacts, particularly in vulnerable regions. This includes:

- **Early Warning Systems**: Projects that develop early warning systems for climate-related hazards, such as floods, droughts, and heatwaves, to enable timely evacuations and mitigate the impact of these events.
- **Climate Change Education and Awareness**: Initiatives that promote climate change education and awareness among communities, particularly in developing countries, to enhance their understanding of climate change risks and opportunities for adaptation.

## Conclusion

The Green Climate Fund's funding priorities for 2021 focus on projects that address the most pressing climate-related challenges in agriculture, energy, water, sanitation, and climate change adaptation and resilience. By supporting these initiatives, the GCF aims to mobilize $100 billion annually by 2020 to support climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts in developing countries.

# Pressures on Land and Natural Resources

## Increased Poverty

Pressures on land and natural resources, plus increasing poverty have led to increased pressure on livestock riding as pastoralists seek to secure bride wealth payments (Kipui and Riopaweit, 2005).

## Drought and Long-Term Adaptive Strategies

In northern Kenya a range of coping strategies for responding to drought and longer-term adaptive strategies can be identified (Morton, 2007), including:

1. **Multi-species herds to exploit different ecological niches**
2. **The combined labour of men, women and children**
3. **Informal savings and credit mechanisms through shopkeepers and bank accounts**
4. **Use of supplementary feed for livestock and interspecification of animal disease management via both indigenous and scientific methods**
5. **Payment for water from powered boreholes**
6. **Diversification of crops and livestock production**
7. **Inter-cropping only mechanisms for sharing livestock products**
