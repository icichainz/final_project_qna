# Detailed Project / Programme Description

## Objective

The objective of this project is to develop and implement a strategic water transfer scheme in the Salis Plain. The project aims to increase climate resilience of irrigation infrastructure, improve institutional capacity for resilience of irrigation water infrastructure, and enhance the management and oversight of irrigation water infrastructure.

## Scope

The scope of this project includes the following activities:

1. Strengthening institutional capacity for resilience of irrigation water infrastructure
2. Improving the management and oversight of irrigation water infrastructure
3. Enhancing the management and oversight of irrigation water infrastructure
4. Developing an operational project plan
5. Implementing the operational project plan
6. Monitoring and reporting on the progress of the project
7. Ensuring that the project is financed through technical and institutional capacity building in the Salis Plain
8. Ensuring that the project is implemented in accordance with the Ministry of Agriculture's guidelines and regulations
9. Ensuring that the project is implemented in a sustainable manner, taking into account environmental and social impacts
10. Ensuring that the project is implemented in a transparent and accountable manner, with clear communication and reporting mechanisms.

## Deliverables

The deliverables of this project include:

1. A detailed operational project plan
2. A monitoring and reporting plan
3. A financial report on the progress of the project
4. A final report on the outcomes of the project
5. A sustainability report on the environmental and social impacts of the project
6. A stakeholder engagement plan
7. A risk management plan
8. A quality assurance plan
9. A procurement plan
10. A human resource development plan.

## Budget

The budget for this project is EUR 500,000. The budget will be allocated as follows:

1. Operational project plan: EUR 200,000
2. Monitoring and reporting: EUR 100,000
3. Financial report: EUR 100,000
4. Final report: EUR 100,000
5. Sustainability report: EUR 100,000
6. Stakeholder engagement plan: EUR 100,000
7. Risk management plan: EUR 100,000
8. Quality assurance plan: EUR 100,000
9. Procurement plan: EUR 100,000
10. Human resource development plan: EUR 100,000.

# Detailed Project / Programme Description

## Object / Programme Description

The objective of this project is to promote effective community involvement in water governance and improve awareness of climate resilience issues among end users of water services, and gender equality. This sub-component will be delivered by the following activities:

1. **Community Involvement in Irrigation Governance**: This sub-component aims to strengthen capacity for demand management methods, thus enhancing the project's scope. The intake is a parallel investment funded by SEDF and will connect to this pipeline construction in the right bank sub-structure of the BDC dam.

2. **Stakeholder Engagement Activities**: This sub-component will deliver stakeholder engagement activities, including education campaigns to raise customer awareness, involving society organizations, large water consumers, technical agencies, and other developmental organizations. The aim is to design specific information campaigns about climate change resilience issues that will be disseminated via various media channels selected to ensure maximum reach.

3. **Facilitation of Dialogue Between End Users of Water Services**: This sub-component aims to facilitate dialogue between end users of water services and the ODP/MAMF focus groups, which will be established in the project area. The aim is to encourage the adoption of more efficient and sustainable irrigation practices by water users (e.g., both male and female farmers), and to encourage improved tariff collection rates by the Authorities.

4. **Promotion of Effective Community Involvement in Water Governance**: This sub-component will deliver community involvement in water governance, including the implementation of a new Water Law and improved awareness of climate resilience through two complementary activities covering: (a) stakeholder engagement initiatives to increase knowledge about sustainable water use and effective adaptation options; and (b) education campaigns to raise customer awareness.
