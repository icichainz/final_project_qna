## Funding proposal package for FP101

### Summary

This addendum contains the following seven parts:

#### a) A funding proposal titled "Resilient Rural Belize" (the "Resilient");

* Reduced vulnerability of smallholder farmers to climate change impacts

## Proposal name: Resilient Rural Belize (Re-Resilient)

## Accredited entity: International Fund for Agricultural development (IFAD)

### Impact potential

The Independent Technical Advisory Panel (ITAP) has assessed the impact potential of the proposed project, "Resilient Rural Belize (Re-Resilient)," in line with the guidelines provided by the International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD). The assessment is based on the following factors:

1. **Climate Change Adaptation**: The project aims to enhance the resilience of rural communities to climate change impacts, such as increased frequency and intensity of extreme weather events. This will be achieved through the implementation of climate-smart agricultural practices and early warning systems.
2. **Sustainable Development**: The project is designed to promote sustainable development by improving access to markets for local products, enhancing rural incomes, and reducing poverty. This will be achieved through the promotion of value chains and the development of market linkages.
3. **Social Equity**: The project aims to reduce gender inequality and improve the social status of marginalized groups by providing them with access to education, healthcare, and other essential services.
4. **Economic Viability**: The project is expected to be economically viable through the generation of income from the sale of local products and the provision of financial services to rural communities.
5. **Institutional Strengthening**: The project aims to strengthen the institutional capacity of rural communities by promoting participatory decision-making processes and the development of local governance structures.
