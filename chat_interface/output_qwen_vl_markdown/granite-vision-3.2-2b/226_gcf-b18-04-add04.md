 Output 1.1 
 Under the risk reduction component, participants build or recover assets that reduce the impacts of climate shocks 
 and help food insecure households and communities to adapt to climate change. 
 Participatory planning and watershed based approaches are used to develop and implement 
 soil and water conservation measures and related assets for cultivation through the removal of sand, the construction of 
 dams, irrigation canals, ponds, reservoirs, etc., in order to improve the resilience of farmers and their livelihoods. 
 WFP enables households to invest in maker tools, fertilizers and new technologies to increase their agricultural 
 productivity at the local level, gradually making them able to adapt to climate change. 
 Savings help build a buffer against short-term needs and idiosyncratic shocks, 
 such as rainfall fluctuations or market price changes. 
 The activities occur at the household level where members of 
 the same household participate in the design and implementation of different 
 actions to improve their livelihoods. Each member deposits his/her contributions to the creation of rural financial markets, by building 
 micro-finance institutions and gradually transitioning 
 them into cooperatives. </doc>
