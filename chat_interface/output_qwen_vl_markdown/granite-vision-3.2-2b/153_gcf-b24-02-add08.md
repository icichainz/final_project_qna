Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to submit a funding proposal for the GCF by Kreditanstalt für Wiederaufbau (KfW) regarding Blue Action Fund (BAF) - GCF Ecosystem Based Adaptation Programme. The purpose of this proposal is to outline the objectives, activities, and expected outcomes of the proposed programme.

## Background

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is a global fund established by the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) to support developing countries in their efforts to mitigate and adapt to climate change. The GCF has been instrumental in financing various projects, including those related to renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, and disaster risk reduction.

The Blue Action Fund (BAF) is a programme under the GCF that focuses on supporting countries in their efforts to adapt to climate change through the implementation of ecosystem-based adaptation measures. The BAF aims to enhance the resilience of communities and ecosystems to the impacts of climate change, while also promoting sustainable development and economic growth.

## Objectives

The primary objective of this funding proposal is to support the implementation of the GCF Ecosystem Based Adaptation Programme in Mozambique. The programme aims to enhance the resilience of communities and ecosystems to the impacts of climate change, while also promoting sustainable development and economic growth.

## Activities

The proposed activities for this funding proposal include:

1. Conducting a comprehensive assessment of the current state of ecosystem-based adaptation measures in Mozambique.
2. Developing a national action plan to enhance the resilience of communities and ecosystems to the impacts of climate change.
3. Providing technical assistance and capacity building to local stakeholders, including government agencies, community organizations, and private sector entities.
4. Promoting the development of innovative and sustainable solutions for ecosystem-based adaptation measures.
5. Monitoring and evaluating the progress of the programme and reporting on its outcomes to the GCF and other relevant stakeholders.

## Expected Outcomes

The expected outcomes of this funding proposal include:

1. Enhanced resilience of communities and ecosystems to the impacts of climate change in Mozambique.
2. Promotion of sustainable development and economic growth in the region.
3. Increased awareness and understanding of ecosystem-based adaptation measures among local stakeholders.
4. Development of innovative and sustainable solutions for ecosystem-based adaptation measures.
5. Improved monitoring and evaluation of the progress of the programme and its outcomes.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, this funding proposal aims to support the implementation of the GCF Ecosystem Based Adaptation Programme in Mozambique. The proposed activities and expected outcomes are designed to enhance the resilience of communities and ecosystems to the impacts of climate change, while also promoting sustainable development and economic growth in the region.

We believe that this funding proposal will contribute significantly to the achievement of the GCF's objectives and the realization of its mandate to support developing countries in their efforts to mitigate and adapt to climate change.
