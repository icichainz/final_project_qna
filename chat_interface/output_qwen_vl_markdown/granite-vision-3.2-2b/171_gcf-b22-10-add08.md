1. Mobilize private sector investment in climate change mitigation and adaptation projects.
2. Support the implementation of low-carbon technologies and practices in developing countries.
3. Enhance the capacity of developing countries to implement climate change mitigation and adaptation projects.
4. Contribute to the achievement of the Paris Agreement goals on climate change.

The scope of the GCF project includes:

1. Developing countries with a high potential for low-carbon technologies and practices.
2. Sectors such as energy, transportation, industry, and agriculture.
