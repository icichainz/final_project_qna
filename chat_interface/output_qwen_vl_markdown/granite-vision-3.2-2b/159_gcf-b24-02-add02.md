\[ \text{IRR} = -\frac{\text{NPV}}{\text{CFT}} \]

## III. Estimated Net Present Value (NPV)

It represents the difference between the present value of all cash inflows and outflows over the life of the project. The NPV is calculated using the following formula:

\[ \text{NPV} = \sum_{t=0}^{T} \frac{CF_t}{(1 + r)^t} - C_0 \]

where CF_t is the cash flow at time t, r is the discount rate, and C_0 is the initial investment.

## IV. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Analysis

The DCF analysis is a method of valuing an investment or project by estimating its future free cash flows and discounting them back to their present value using a discount rate. The DCF analysis is calculated using the following formula:

\[ \text{Value} = \sum_{t=0}^{T} \frac{CF_t}{(1 + r)^t} - C_0 \]

where CF_t is the cash flow at time t, r is the discount rate, and C_0 is the initial investment.
