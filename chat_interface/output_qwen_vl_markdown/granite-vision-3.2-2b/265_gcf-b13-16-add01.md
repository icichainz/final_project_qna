     cooperative societies, which include 48 credit unions and 95

     partnership or through a joint venture.

     economy in El Salvador is USD 61.1% of GDP at current prices. Within this 
     category, the largest TA is held by the private sector, which 
     comprises 87.4% of the TA and accounts for approximately 
     USD 30.2 billion (USD 29.5 billion in 2012). The second-largest TA is 
 held by the public sector, which comprises 12.6% of the TA and accounts for approximately 
     USD 17.4 billion (USD 18.3 billion in 2012). The third-largest TA is 
 held by foreign investors, which comprises 1.5% of the TA and accounts for approximately 
     USD 6.9 billion (USD 7.2 billion in 2012). The fourth-largest TA is held by the 
 government sector, which comprises 0.4% of the TA and accounts for approximately 
     USD 3.5 billion (USD 3.8 billion in 2012). The fifth-largest TA is held by other entities, which 
 comprises 0.6% of the TA and accounts for approximately USD 7.9 billion (USD 8.4 billion in 2012).
