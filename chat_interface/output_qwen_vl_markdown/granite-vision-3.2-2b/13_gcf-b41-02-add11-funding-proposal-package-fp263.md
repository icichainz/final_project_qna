# Gender and Social Inclusion Action Plan

## IMPACT STATEMENT
If the appropriate investment capital and technical assistance are provided to MSLF, Z staff and investees, THEN they will be able to engage women in their workforce and value chains contributing to women's economic empowerment and to social inclusion. This will lead to increased capacity of MSLF 2 investee companies to operate and to align with global gender equality commitments.

## OUTPUT STATEMENT
Finance and technical assistance lead to increased capacity of MSLF 2 investee companies to engage women beneficiaries, to hire women to thrive in workforce and value chains, to support investment companies to align with industry standards and best practices, to generate and disseminate knowledge about best practices for women activities across geographies.

## MSLF2 Gender Objectives:
1. Reach women beneficiaries and involve women at all levels of the workforce and value chains.
2. Ensure that Z staff and investees are able to engage women in their workforce and value chains contributing to women's economic empowerment and to social inclusion.
3. Support investment companies to align with industry standards and best practices.
4. Generate and disseminate knowledge about best practices for women activities across geographies.
