The organization of the United Nations for Agriculture and Food Security (FAO) was established in 1961 to promote food security and sustainable agricultural development. The FAO is responsible for providing technical assistance, policy advice, and capacity building to its member states. It also plays a key role in promoting international cooperation on agricultural issues.

## Gender Equality and the Environment

Gender equality is an important issue that is closely linked to environmental sustainability. The FAO recognizes the importance of addressing gender disparities in order to achieve sustainable development goals, including those related to climate change mitigation and adaptation.

The FAO has developed a Gender Assessment and Action Plan (FAO-GAAP) to address gender issues in its work. The plan outlines specific actions that the FAO will take to promote gender equality and sustainable development. These actions include:

1. Promoting women's participation in decision-making processes related to agriculture and food security.
2. Addressing gender disparities in access to education, healthcare, and economic opportunities.
3. Encouraging the integration of gender considerations into national policies and programs.
4. Supporting women's leadership in research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
5. Promoting gender-sensitive agricultural practices and technologies.
