# Funding Proposal

## Project Information

- **Project Title:** Climate change adaptation solutions for Local Authorities in the Federated States of Micronesia
- **Country:** Federated States of Micronesia
- **Accredited Entity:** Pacific Community (SPC)
- **Date of first submission:** 2021/08/29
- **Date of current submission:** 2021/08/29
- **Version number:** V.4

## Funding Proposal Details

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is seeking funding proposals for projects that address climate change adaptation solutions in the Federated States of Micronesia. The project aims to support local authorities in implementing measures to mitigate and adapt to the impacts of climate change.

## Project Description

The proposed project will focus on developing and implementing climate change adaptation strategies for local authorities in the Federated States of Micronesia. This includes projects that address issues such as sea level rise, increased frequency of extreme weather events, and changes in precipitation patterns. The project will also support the development of early warning systems and disaster risk reduction measures to enhance the resilience of local communities to climate change impacts.

## Project Budget

The proposed budget for this project is $10 million over a period of five years, with funding allocated as follows:

- Year 1: $2 million for project planning and preparation
- Year 2: $3 million for implementation of adaptation measures
- Year 3: $4 million for monitoring and evaluation of the project's impacts
- Year 4: $3 million for dissemination of findings and best practices
- Year 5: $1 million for final reporting and follow-up activities

## Project Timeline

The proposed project timeline is as follows:

- Year 1: Project planning and preparation (Month 1-6)
- Year 2: Implementation of adaptation measures (Month 7-30)
- Year 3: Monitoring and evaluation of the project's impacts (Month 31-96)
- Year 4: Dissemination of findings and best practices (Month 97-120)
- Year 5: Final reporting and follow-up activities (Month 121-180)
