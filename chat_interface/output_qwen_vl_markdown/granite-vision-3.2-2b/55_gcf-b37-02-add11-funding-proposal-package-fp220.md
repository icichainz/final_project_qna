### B.2. Project Programme Information

### B.2.1. Project Description

The ARGCAFM mechanism will provide financial support for the implementation of adaptation measures in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda. The target countries are low income countries with high vulnerability to climate change impacts.

### B.2.2. Project Objectives

1. To assess the vulnerability of the target countries to climate change impacts.
2. To identify the economic potential and capacity to implement adaptation measures in the target countries.
3. To provide financial support for the implementation of adaptation measures in the target countries.
4. To promote sustainable development and reduce greenhouse gas emissions in the target countries.

### B.2.3. Project Activities

1. Conduct a vulnerability assessment of the target countries to climate change impacts.
2. Identify the economic potential and capacity to implement adaptation measures in the target countries.
3. Provide financial support for the implementation of adaptation measures in the target countries.
4. Promote sustainable development and reduce greenhouse gas emissions in the target countries.

### B.2.4. Project Timeline

The ARGCAFM mechanism will provide financial support for the implementation of adaptation measures in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda over a period of 5 years. The project will be implemented in phases, with the first phase starting in 2023 and ending in 2027.

### B.2.5. Project Budget

The ARGCAFM mechanism will provide financial support for the implementation of adaptation measures in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda. The budget for each phase of the project is as follows:

* Phase 1: $10 million
* Phase 2: $15 million
* Phase 3: $20 million
* Phase 4: $25 million
* Phase 5: $30 million

### B.2.6. Project Partners

The ARGCAFM mechanism will be implemented by the following partners:

* Green Climate Fund (GCF)
* Kenya Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources
* Uganda Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources
* Tanzania Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources
* Rwanda Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources

### B.2.7. Project Monitoring and Evaluation

The ARGCAFM mechanism will be implemented with a monitoring and evaluation framework to ensure that the project is being implemented effectively and efficiently. The monitoring and evaluation framework will include:

* Regular progress reports from the project partners
* Independent evaluations of the project's implementation
* Feedback from stakeholders, including local communities and civil society organizations

### B.2.8. Project Reporting

The ARGCAFM mechanism will provide regular updates on the progress of the project to the GCF and other stakeholders. The reporting will include:

* Progress reports on the implementation of adaptation measures in the target countries
* Financial reports on the use of financial support for the implementation of adaptation measures
* Feedback from stakeholders, including local communities and civil society organizations

### B.2.9. Project Closure

The ARGCAFM mechanism will be implemented until 2030, with a final report submitted to the GCF in 2031. The final report will include:

* A summary of the project's progress and achievements
* Recommendations for future adaptation measures in the target countries
* Lessons learned from the implementation of the ARGCAFM mechanism
