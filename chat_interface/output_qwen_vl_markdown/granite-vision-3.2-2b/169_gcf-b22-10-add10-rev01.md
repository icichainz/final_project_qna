# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum X

## Funding proposal package for FP106

### Summary

This addendum contains the following six parts:

#### a) A funding proposal summary titled "Embedded Generation Investment Programme"

#### b) No objection letter issued by the national designated authority(s) or local point(s).

#### c) Environmental and social report(s) disclosure.

#### d) Response from the accredited entity to the Independent Technical Advisory Panel's assessment, and

#### e) Gender documentation of the funding proposal.

#### f) Information Disclosure Policy of the Fund, the funding proposal titled "Embedded Generation Investment Programme" is hereby submitted by the accredited entity to the national designated authority(s) or local point(s), respectively. Pursuant to the Comprehensive Framework for Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation (COP21), the funding proposal is intended to be used as a basis for the development of a climate change mitigation and adaptation strategy in Southern Africa, which will be circulated on a limited distribution basis only to Board Members or Alternate Board Members to ensure confidentiality of certain proprietary, legally privileged

or commercially sensitive information of the entity.
