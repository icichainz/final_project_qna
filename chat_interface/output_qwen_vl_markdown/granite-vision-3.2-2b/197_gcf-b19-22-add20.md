# Letter of No Objection to the Green Climate Fund (GCF) for the Aumen Resilient Agriculture Fund (ARAF) by Aumen Fund

## Date: May 10, 2017

## To: The Executive Director

## From: Ministry of Finance, Planning & Economic Development

## Subject: Letter of No Objection to the Green Climate Fund (GCF) for the Aumen Resilient Agriculture Fund (ARAF) by Aumen Fund

## Dear Sir/Madam,

We are writing to express our views on the proposal submitted by the Aumen Fund regarding the allocation of funds from the Green Climate Fund (GCF) to the Aumen Resilient Agriculture Fund (ARAF). We have reviewed the proposal and would like to provide a letter of no objection.

## Background

The Aumen Resilient Agriculture Fund (ARAF) is a proposed initiative by the Aumen Fund, which aims to enhance the resilience of agricultural systems in developing countries. The GCF is a global fund established under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) to support climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts in developing countries.

## Our Position

We believe that the proposal submitted by the Aumen Fund for the allocation of funds from the GCF to the ARAF is well-founded and aligns with the objectives of both the GCF and the Aumen Fund. The proposed allocation of funds will support the implementation of climate change adaptation measures in developing countries, which are critical for enhancing food security and reducing poverty.

## Conclusion

We would like to express our appreciation for the proposal submitted by the Aumen Fund and acknowledge its alignment with the objectives of both the GCF and the Aumen Fund. We believe that the proposed allocation of funds from the GCF to the ARAF will support the implementation of climate change adaptation measures in developing countries, which are critical for enhancing food security and reducing poverty.
