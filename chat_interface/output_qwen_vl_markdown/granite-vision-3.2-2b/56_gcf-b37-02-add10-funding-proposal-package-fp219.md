## Introduction

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is a global initiative that aims to mobilize $100 billion annually by 2020 to support climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts in developing countries. This proposal outlines the funding requirements for the GCF's next cycle, which will run from 2023 to 2027.

## Funding Requirements

The GCF provides funding to eligible projects that meet certain criteria, including:

1. **Climate Change Mitigation**: Projects must address climate change and its impacts, such as reducing greenhouse gas emissions, enhancing carbon sequestration, or promoting sustainable land use practices.
2. **Adaptation**: Projects must also address the impacts of climate change on vulnerable populations, such as sea-level rise, droughts, or extreme weather events.
3. **Economic Viability**: Projects must be economically viable and have a clear plan for implementation and monitoring.
4. **Transparency and Accountability**: Projects must demonstrate transparency in their operations and reporting, and be subject to independent verification.

## Funding Allocation

The GCF will allocate funding based on the following criteria:
