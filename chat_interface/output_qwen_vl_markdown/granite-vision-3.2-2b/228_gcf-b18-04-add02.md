   - Focus on wind power, solar PV, and biomass energy.
   - Targeted sectors include residential, commercial, and industrial sectors.
   - Project duration: 5 years (2017-2021).

# Annex 3: BURNOYE SPP PROJECT

## Introduction

The Burmoye Solar project (LUP) is a joint venture between Green LP and Kazakh-UK entities. The project aims to pioneer the use of non-recourse project finance structure in the energy sector, particularly in the Zharmbiy region of southern Kazakhstan.

## Project Overview

The Burmoye Solar project was initiated in 2014 as part of the Renewable Energy Law. The project is located in the Zharmbiy region, which is a significant energy reserve area in Kazakhstan. The project's objective is to generate renewable energy and reduce reliance on fossil fuels.

## Project Financing

The project was financed through a combination of equity and debt instruments. Green LP provided the initial equity investment, while the CFT contributed the remaining debt. The total amount invested in the project is USD 14.6 million (USD 9.75 million equity + USD 4.85 million debt).

## Project Implementation

The project involves the construction of a solar power plant, which will generate electricity and provide energy security to the region. The project also includes the development of a community center and other infrastructure facilities.

## Project Timeline

The project is expected to be completed by 2025. The project timeline includes the following phases:

1. Feasibility studies and permitting
2. Construction of the solar power plant
3. Development of the community center and other infrastructure facilities
4. Commissioning and operation of the project

## Project Risks

The project faces several risks, including political instability, regulatory changes, and technical challenges. The project team has developed a risk management plan to mitigate these risks and ensure the successful completion of the project.

## Conclusion

The Burmoye Solar project is a significant investment in renewable energy and non-recourse project finance structure in Kazakhstan. The project aims to generate renewable energy, reduce reliance on fossil fuels, and provide energy security to the region. The project team has developed a risk management plan to mitigate potential risks and ensure the successful completion of the project.
