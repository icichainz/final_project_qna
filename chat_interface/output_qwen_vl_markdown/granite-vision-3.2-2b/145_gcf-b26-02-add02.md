## Revised Proposal for the Green Climate Fund (GCF) by the Ministry of Finance of Indonesia

## I. Introduction

The Ministry of Finance of Indonesia, in accordance with the provisions of the Act No. 175/2014 on the Green Climate Fund (GCF), hereby submits a revised proposal for the GCF by the Ministry of Finance of Indonesia for the fiscal year 2019-2020.

## II. Purpose

The purpose of this proposal is to provide funding for projects that align with the objectives of the GCF, which are to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and enhance climate resilience in Indonesia. The proposed projects will focus on areas such as renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, and disaster risk reduction.

## III. Project Details

The following projects have been selected for funding:

1. **Renewable Energy Projects**
   - Project Name: Solar Power Plant in Bali
   - Location: Bali, Indonesia
   - Expected Output: 50 MW of solar power capacity
   - Funding Amount: IDR 2,000,000,000

2. **Sustainable Agriculture Projects**
   - Project Name: Organic Farming in West Sumatra
   - Location: West Sumatra, Indonesia
   - Expected Output: Increased organic farming area by 10%
   - Funding Amount: IDR 1,500,000,000

3. **Disaster Risk Reduction Projects**
   - Project Name: Flood Control in Jakarta
   - Location: Jakarta, Indonesia
   - Expected Output: Improved flood control infrastructure by 20%
   - Funding Amount: IDR 1,200,000,000

## IV. Implementation Plan

The proposed projects will be implemented in the following phases:

1. **Phase 1 (Months 1-3):** Project planning and preparation
2. **Phase 2 (Months 4-6):** Construction of project facilities
3. **Phase 3 (Months 7-9):** Implementation of project activities
4. **Phase 4 (Months 10-12):** Monitoring and evaluation
5. **Phase 5 (Months 13-15):** Reporting and disbursement of funds

## V. Conclusion

The Ministry of Finance of Indonesia is committed to providing funding for projects that align with the objectives of the GCF. The proposed projects are expected to contribute significantly to reducing greenhouse gas emissions and enhancing climate resilience in Indonesia.
