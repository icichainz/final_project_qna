## Trend in electricity generation:

Power demand has increased drastically over the last decade, 2006 to 544.827 in 2015. Power consumption doubled from 2006 to 2015, with a record average of 340 hours in 2015, compared to 180 hours.

## Challenges

The country's limited supply of electricity is one of the most significant challenges. The cost of electricity remains high, and the country imports on average more energy than it produces. The transformation of the agricultural sector and the rural environment requires new programs, including off-grid access solutions.
