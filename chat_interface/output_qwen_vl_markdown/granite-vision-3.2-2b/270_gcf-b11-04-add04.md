# Funding Proposal

## Version 1.0

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is seeking high-quality funding proposals. Accredited entities are expected to develop their funding proposals, in close consultation with the relevant national designated authority, with due consideration of the GCF's investment framework and Results Management Framework. The final decision on the funding proposals will be made by the GCF Board of Trustees based on the criteria outlined in this proposal.

## Proposal Details

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is seeking high-quality funding proposals. Accredited entities are expected to develop their funding proposals, in close consultation with the relevant national designated authority, with due consideration of the GCF's investment framework and Results Management Framework. The final decision on the funding proposals will be made by the GCF Board of Trustees based on the criteria outlined in this proposal.

## Proposal Submission Guidelines

1. **Proposal Format**: Proposals should be submitted electronically via the GCF online portal. Each proposal must be a single PDF file, no larger than 5MB.
2. **Proposal Content**: Proposals should include:
   - A cover page with the title "Funding Proposal" and the version number (1.0).
   - A table of contents listing all sections of the proposal.
   - An executive summary (maximum 100 words) that provides an overview of the proposed funding project.
   - Detailed description of the proposed funding project, including:
     - Project objectives and expected outcomes.
     - Budget breakdown, including direct costs and indirect costs.
     - Implementation plan, including timelines and milestones.
     - Monitoring and evaluation plan, including indicators and data collection methods.
     - Risk management plan, including contingency measures.
   - A detailed budget justification, explaining how the proposed funding will be used to achieve the project objectives.
   - A list of supporting documents, such as letters of support from relevant stakeholders or financial statements for the proposed project.
3. **Proposal Submission**: Proposals must be submitted electronically via the GCF online portal by [insert deadline]. Late submissions will not be accepted.
4. **Evaluation Criteria**: Proposals will be evaluated based on the following criteria:
   - Alignment with the GCF's investment framework and Results Management Framework.
   - Feasibility of the proposed funding project.
   - Potential for impact and scalability.
   - Quality of the proposal, including clarity, completeness, and adherence to the submission guidelines.
5. **Proposal Review**: The GCF Board of Trustees will review all proposals submitted electronically via the online portal. The final decision on the funding proposals will be made by the GCF Board of Trustees based on the criteria outlined in this proposal.
6. **Proposal Feedback**: Proposals that are selected for further consideration will receive feedback from the GCF Board of Trustees. The feedback will include comments on the proposal's strengths and areas for improvement.
7. **Proposal Implementation**: If a proposal is selected, the GCF will provide funding to support the implementation of the proposed funding project. The GCF will also work with the accredited entity to ensure that the proposed funding project is implemented in accordance with the agreed-upon terms and conditions.
8. **Proposal Reporting**: Accredited entities are required to submit regular progress reports on the implementation of the proposed funding project. The GCF will review these reports to ensure compliance with the agreed-upon terms and conditions.
9. **Proposal Closure**: Once the proposed funding project is completed, the accredited entity must submit a final report detailing the outcomes of the project. The GCF will review this report to determine whether the proposed funding project has achieved its objectives.
10. **Proposal Review Process**: The GCF Board of Trustees will review all proposals submitted electronically via the online portal. The final decision on the funding proposals will be made by the GCF Board of Trustees based on the criteria outlined in this proposal.
