# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum XIX

## Funding proposal package for FP077

### Summary

This addendum contains the following three parts:

1. **A) A funding proposal summary (including a description of the project, its objectives, and the expected outcomes).**
2. **B) No objection letters issued by the national designated authority(ies) or local point(s) of reference.**
3. **c) Environmental and social report(s) disclosure.**

### Part 1: A) Funding proposal summary

The funding proposal is for a project titled "Green Climate Fund - Addendum XIX" (FP077). The project aims to support the implementation of climate change mitigation and adaptation measures in vulnerable communities. The expected outcomes include reduced greenhouse gas emissions, improved energy efficiency, and enhanced disaster resilience.

### Part 2: B) No objection letters issued by the national designated authority(ies) or local point(s) of reference

No objection letters have been issued by the national designated authority(ies) or local point(s) of reference regarding this funding proposal.

### Part 3: c) Environmental and social report disclosure

The environmental and social report for this funding proposal is attached as Appendix A. The report includes a detailed assessment of the project's potential impacts on the environment, society, and economy. It also outlines the measures taken to ensure compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

### Conclusion

This addendum provides a comprehensive overview of the funding proposal for FP077, including a summary of the project, no objection letters issued by national authorities or local point(s) of reference, and an environmental and social report disclosure.
