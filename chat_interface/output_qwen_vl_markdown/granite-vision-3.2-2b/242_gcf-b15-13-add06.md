## Summary

This addendum contains the following three parts:

1. A funding proposal titled "Accelerating the Transformational Shift to a Low-Carbon Economy in the Republic of Mauritius" submitted by UNDP;
2. An economic analysis and forecast prepared by the Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Environment and Climate Change; and
3. A summary of the findings and recommendations of the economic analysis and forecast.

The documents are presented as submitted by the accredited entity, and national designated authority or focal point, respectively.

# No-objection letter issued by the national designated authority

## MINISTRY OF FINANCE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

## Date: 21 October 2016

## Letter of No-objection to the revised proposal of the GCF Project, "Accelerating the Transformational Shift to a Low-Carbon Economy in the Republic of Mauritania"

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to inform you that we have received your letter of no-objection to the revised proposal of the Green Climate Fund (GCF) Project, "Accelerating the Transformational Shift to a Low-Carbon Economy in the Republic of Mauritania". We appreciate your time and consideration.

## Background

The GCF Project aims to support the transition of the Republic of Mauritania towards a low-carbon economy by promoting sustainable development, reducing greenhouse gas emissions, and enhancing energy efficiency. The project is in line with the objectives of the Paris Agreement and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

## Review Process

The GCF Project underwent a rigorous review process by the Green Climate Fund Secretariat, which included an assessment of the project's feasibility, potential impacts on the environment and society, and alignment with the GCF's objectives. The review process was conducted in accordance with the GCF's guidelines and procedures.

## Findings

Based on the review process, we have found that the GCF Project is consistent with the GCF's objectives and principles. The project aligns with the Paris Agreement and the SDGs, and it has the potential to contribute significantly to the transition towards a low-carbon economy in Mauritania.

## Recommendations

We recommend that you approve the revised proposal of the GCF Project as submitted by the applicant. We believe that the project is well-designed and has the potential to make a significant contribution to the transition towards a low-carbon economy in Mauritania.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, we have reviewed the revised proposal of the GCF Project and found that it is consistent with the GCF's objectives and principles. We recommend that you approve the project as submitted by the applicant.
