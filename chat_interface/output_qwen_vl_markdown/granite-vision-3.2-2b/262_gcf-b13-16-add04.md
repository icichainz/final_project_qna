# Detailed Project / Programme Description

## Objective
The objective of this project is to provide reliable and accurate meteorological observations including: 19 synoptic data.

## Scope
This project aims to improve the quality of weather forecasting in Mali by providing more frequent and accurate information on temperature, precipitation, and other weather parameters. The project will also focus on improving the capacity of local meteorological stations to provide reliable weather information to the population.

## Deliverables
The deliverables of this project include:
- Improved weather forecasting capabilities in Mali
- Increased availability of weather data for decision making by government agencies and private sector companies
- Enhanced capacity of local meteorological stations to provide reliable weather information to the population

## Budget
The budget for this project is $10 million over a period of 5 years. The budget will be allocated as follows:
- Personnel costs: $2 million
- Equipment and infrastructure costs: $3 million
- Training and capacity building costs: $2 million
- Monitoring and evaluation costs: $1 million

## Timeline
The project is expected to be completed within 5 years, with the following milestones:
- Year 1: Completion of personnel recruitment and training
- Year 2: Equipment and infrastructure installation
- Year 3: Training and capacity building
- Year 4: Monitoring and evaluation
- Year 5: Project completion

## Risk Management
The project is subject to several risks, including:
- Climate change impacts on agriculture and water resources
- Political instability in Mali
- Limited access to funding and technical expertise
- Inadequate infrastructure for monitoring and evaluation

To mitigate these risks, the project will implement the following measures:
- Conduct regular risk assessments and develop contingency plans
- Build partnerships with local stakeholders and international organizations
- Invest in capacity building and training programs
- Develop a robust monitoring and evaluation framework

## Conclusion
The detailed project proposal outlines the proposed activities, budget, timeline, and risk management measures for the Mali Meteo project. The project aims to provide reliable and accurate weather forecasts and climate information to support decision-making in agriculture, water resources, and other sectors.
