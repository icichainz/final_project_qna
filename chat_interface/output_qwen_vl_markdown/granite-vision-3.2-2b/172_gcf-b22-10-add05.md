# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum V

## Funding proposal package for FP103

### Summary

This addendum contains the following seven parts:

1. **A funding proposal titled 'Promotion of Climate-Friendly Cooking in Kenya and Senegal'**
2. A local elected officer issued by the national designated authority(ies) or local government body
3. Environmental and social report(s) of disclosure, including:
   - Description of the project
   - Expected outcomes and benefits
   - Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
   - Monitoring and evaluation plan
4. Secretariat's assessment
5. Independent Technical Advisory Panel's assessment
6. Response of the accredited entity to the independent Technical Advisory Panel assessment, and
7. Gender dissemination plan

We are writing to express our no objection to the funding proposal for the GCF by "Green Promotion of Climate - Friendly Cooking: Kenya and Senegal" project in Senegal. The proposal is consistent with the objectives of the GCF and aligns with the national priorities, strategies, and plans of Senegal.

## Background

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is a global fund established by the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) to support developing countries in their efforts to mitigate and adapt to climate change. The GCF has provided funding for various projects, including those related to sustainable development, renewable energy, and climate resilience.

## Project Overview

The "Green Promotion of Climate - Friendly Cooking: Kenya and Senegal" project aims to promote the use of friendly cooking methods in Kenya and Senegal, which are both countries with high levels of greenhouse gas emissions. The project will focus on promoting sustainable food systems, reducing food waste, and increasing access to affordable and nutritious food for vulnerable populations.

## Funding Proposal

The funding proposal submitted by the Green Climate Fund proposes a total of $10 million over five years to support the project. The proposed activities include:

1. Conducting a feasibility study on the potential impact of friendly cooking methods in Kenya and Senegal.
2. Developing a national policy framework for promoting friendly cooking methods.
3. Providing training and capacity building programs for farmers, food processors, and other stakeholders.
4. Establishing a monitoring and evaluation system to track the progress of the project.
5. Promoting the use of friendly cooking methods in public spaces and events.

## Conclusion

We are pleased to express our no objection to the funding proposal for the GCF by "Green Promotion of Climate - Friendly Cooking: Kenya and Senegal" project in Senegal. We believe that this project aligns with the objectives of the GCF and contributes to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) related to sustainable food systems, climate resilience, and poverty reduction.

# GCF Funding proposal "Promotion of Climate-Friendly Cooking in Kenya and Senegal" Gender Assessment - 22 January 2019

## Introduction

The Government of Kenya and the Government of Senegal have agreed to collaborate on a project titled "Promotion of Climate-Friendly Cooking in Kenya and Senegal." This project aims to promote sustainable cooking practices that reduce greenhouse gas emissions and mitigate climate change.

## Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to promote the use of clean fuels for cooking, such as biomass and biogas, in Kenya and Senegal. The project will also aim to educate households on the benefits of using clean fuels for cooking and how to access these fuels.

## Project Activities

The following activities are planned to be carried out under this project:

1. Conduct a needs assessment to identify the areas where clean fuels can be promoted and the barriers that prevent households from using them.
2. Develop a training program for households on how to use clean fuels for cooking and the benefits of doing so.
3. Establish a network of community-based organizations (CBOs) to promote the use of clean fuels in their communities.
4. Conduct a pilot project in two villages in Kenya and Senegal to test the effectiveness of the training program and the establishment of the CBOs.
5. Monitor and evaluate the impact of the project on households' cooking practices and greenhouse gas emissions.

## Budget

The total budget for this project is $10 million, with 60% allocated to the needs assessment, 20% to the training program, 10% to the establishment of the CBOs, and 10% to monitoring and evaluation.

## Conclusion

This project aims to promote sustainable cooking practices that reduce greenhouse gas emissions and mitigate climate change in Kenya and Senegal. By promoting the use of clean fuels for cooking, this project will help to reduce the country's dependence on fossil fuels and contribute to global efforts to combat climate change.
