## Introduction

The African Development Bank Group (ADBG) is pleased to submit this funding proposal for the Green Climate Fund (GCF) project. This proposal outlines the proposed activities and objectives of the GCF Project, which aims to support climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts in Africa.

## Background

The GCF Project is a key component of the ADB's commitment to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 13: Climate Action. The project focuses on supporting climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts in Africa, with a particular emphasis on vulnerable communities and small-island developing states.

## Proposed Activities

The proposed activities for the GCF Project include:

1. **Climate Change Mitigation**: The project will support the implementation of climate change mitigation measures in African countries, including the adoption of renewable energy sources and the promotion of sustainable land use practices.
2. **Climate Change Adaptation**: The project will support the development and implementation of climate change adaptation measures in African countries, including the construction of climate-resilient infrastructure and the provision of early warning systems for extreme weather events.
3. **Capacity Building**: The project will support capacity building efforts to enhance the knowledge and skills of African policymakers, practitioners, and stakeholders in climate change mitigation and adaptation.
4. **Research and Development**: The project will support research and development activities to advance our understanding of climate change and its impacts on Africa, as well as the development of new technologies and solutions for climate change mitigation and adaptation.

## Objectives

The objectives of the GCF Project are:

1. To reduce greenhouse gas emissions in Africa by 2030, compared to 2005 levels.
2. To enhance the resilience of African communities and ecosystems to climate change impacts.
3. To promote sustainable development and poverty reduction in Africa through climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts.
4. To support the implementation of the Paris Agreement on climate change in Africa.

## Budget

The proposed budget for the GCF Project is as follows:

1. **Climate Change Mitigation**: $50 million
2. **Climate Change Adaptation**: $30 million
3. **Capacity Building**: $20 million
4. **Research and Development**: $10 million

## Conclusion

The African Development Bank Group is committed to supporting the implementation of the GCF Project, which will help to reduce greenhouse gas emissions in Africa and enhance the resilience of African communities and ecosystems to climate change impacts. The proposed activities and objectives align with the Sustainable Development Goals and the Paris Agreement on climate change.
