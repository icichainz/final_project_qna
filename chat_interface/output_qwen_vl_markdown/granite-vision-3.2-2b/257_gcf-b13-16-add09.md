# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum 09

## Funding proposal package for FP017

### Summary

This addendum contains the following four parts:

#### a) A funding proposal summary titled "Climate Action and Solar Energy Development Programme in the Tarapac Region in Chile" submitted by CAF - Banco de Desarrollo.

#### b) An ex-objection letter issued by the national designated authority or local point, and

#### c) Environmental and social report(s) disclosure.

These documents are presented as submitted by the accredited entity and the national designated authority(ies) of a focal point(s), respectively. Pursuant to the Comprehensive Framework Agreement (CFAA) between the Government of Chile, the World Bank, and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), the Ministry of Finance of Chile is responsible for the implementation of the FP017 project.

The Ministry has requested that Alternate Board Members be ensured confidentiality of certain proprietary, legally privileged or commercially sensitive information of the entity.
