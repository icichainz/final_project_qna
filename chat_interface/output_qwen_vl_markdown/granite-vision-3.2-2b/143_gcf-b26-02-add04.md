## Country: Georgia

### Funding Proposal

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is pleased to announce the funding proposal for the project titled "Enabling Implementation of Forest Sector Reform in Georgia to Reduce GHG Emissions from Forest Degradation." This project aims to support the implementation of reforms in the forest sector in Georgia, with a focus on reducing greenhouse gas emissions and promoting sustainable forest management.

### Project Description

The project will involve the following key activities:

1. **Assessment of Current Forest Sector**: Conduct a comprehensive assessment of the current state of the forest sector in Georgia, including an analysis of the drivers of deforestation, the impacts of deforestation on greenhouse gas emissions, and the potential for reform.

2. **Development of Reform Proposal**: Develop a detailed proposal outlining the necessary reforms to be implemented in the forest sector, including changes to land use policies, incentives for sustainable forestry practices, and mechanisms for monitoring and reporting on progress.

3. **Capacity Building**: Provide capacity building programs for government officials, forest managers, and other stakeholders involved in the implementation of the reform proposal. This will include training on best practices for sustainable forest management, as well as workshops on policy development and implementation.

4. **Monitoring and Evaluation**: Establish a monitoring and evaluation framework to track progress towards the goals of the project, including indicators related to greenhouse gas emissions reduction, deforestation rates, and the impacts of reform on sustainable forest management.

5. **Dissemination of Results**: Disseminate the findings of the project through reports, publications, and other means, in order to raise awareness of the importance of forest sector reform and to promote best practices for sustainable forest management.

### Budget

The total budget for this project is $10 million over a period of five years. The budget will be allocated as follows:

- Year 1: Assessment of current forest sector, development of reform proposal, capacity building programs (25% of total budget)
- Year 2: Implementation of reform proposal, monitoring and evaluation (37.5% of total budget)
- Year 3: Monitoring and evaluation, dissemination of results (12.5% of total budget)
- Year 4: Monitoring and evaluation, dissemination of results (12.5% of total budget)
- Year 5: Monitoring and evaluation, dissemination of results (12.5% of total budget)

### Conclusion

The Green Climate Fund is committed to supporting projects that promote sustainable forest management and reduce greenhouse gas emissions from deforestation. The project titled "Enabling Implementation of Forest Sector Reform in Georgia to Reduce GHG Emissions from Forest Degradation" has the potential to make a significant contribution to these goals, and we are pleased to announce this funding proposal for this project.
