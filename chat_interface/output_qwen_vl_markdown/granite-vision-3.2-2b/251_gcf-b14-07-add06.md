# Ministry of Environment and Tourism

## RE: Funding proposal to the Green Climate Fund by the Environmental Investment Fund of Namibia (EIF) regarding "Climate Resilient Agriculture in the three Vulnerable Extreme northern crops growing regions of Namibia"

Dear Sir Maham,

We refer to the project "Climate Resilient Agriculture in the three Vulnerable Extreme northern crops growing regions of Namibia" (Project RE: 0.0610) submitted by the Environmental Investment Fund of Namibia (EIF) on behalf of the Ministry of Environment and Tourism, the National Designated Authority of Namibia.

## Background

The project aims to develop climate-resilient agriculture in the three Vulnerable Extreme northern crops growing regions of Namibia. The project is expected to enhance food security and reduce vulnerability to climate change impacts on agricultural productivity.

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of the project are:

1. To develop and implement climate-resilient agricultural practices in the three Vulnerable Extreme northern crops growing regions of Namibia.
2. To enhance food security and reduce vulnerability to climate change impacts on agricultural productivity.
3. To promote sustainable agriculture practices that are resilient to climate change impacts.
4. To enhance the capacity of local farmers and communities to adapt to climate change impacts.
5. To promote the use of climate-resilient technologies and practices in agricultural production.

## Project Scope

The project will be implemented in the three Vulnerable Extreme northern crops growing regions of Namibia, which are:

1. The Namib Desert Region
2. The Namib River Delta Region
3. The Namib Savanna Region

## Project Timeline

The project is expected to be implemented over a period of five years, from 2016 to 2021.

## Project Budget

The total budget for the project is N$ 50 million (approximately USD 3.7 million).

## Project Partners

The project will involve collaboration between the Environmental Investment Fund of Namibia, the Ministry of Environment and Tourism, the National Designated Authority of Namibia, local farmers and communities, and other relevant stakeholders.

## Conclusion

We believe that the proposed project is a significant step towards enhancing food security and reducing vulnerability to climate change impacts on agricultural productivity in the three Vulnerable Extreme northern crops growing regions of Namibia. We look forward to working with you to implement this project.
