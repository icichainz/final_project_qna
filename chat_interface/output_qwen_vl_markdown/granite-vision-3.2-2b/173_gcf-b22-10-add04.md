<doc> A.2. Project / Programme Executive Summary 
 The main objective of this project is to promote rural electrification through isolated solar photovoltaic systems as a low- 
 cost renewable energy source for the rural population's access to electricity in 70 communities 
 (AMADER, AEER and FER) engaged in rural electrification 
 The project will (i) strengthen the capacity of public institutions (AMADER, AEER and FER) engaged in rural electrification 
 through installation of isolated solar photovoltaic mining systems as a low-cost renewable energy source for the rural population's access to electricity in 70 communities 
 (i.e. Mali), (ii) reduce the cost of electricity generation by 10% and (iii) increase the capacity of electricity generation by 4,83 MW. The project will thus 
 enable Mali to meet its renewable energy and greenhouse gas emission reduction targets with a total capacity of 4,83 MW. The project will thus

## 1. Introduction

The Mali Solar Rural Electrification Project aims to provide clean and reliable electricity to rural communities in Mali, with a focus on gender mainstreaming. This action plan outlines the strategies and actions required to achieve this goal.

## 2. Background

Mali is one of the countries with the lowest levels of electrification in Africa. Rural areas are particularly affected by this issue, as they lack access to electricity for various purposes such as education, healthcare, and economic development. The Mali Solar Rural Electrification Project aims to address these challenges by providing solar power solutions to rural communities.

## 3. Objectives

The primary objective of the Mali Solar Rural Electrification Project is to provide clean and reliable electricity to rural communities in Mali, with a focus on gender mainstreaming. The project aims to achieve this goal by:

1. Providing solar power solutions to rural communities
2. Promoting gender equality and women's empowerment
3. Enhancing the capacity of local institutions and organizations
4. Fostering public-private partnerships
5. Ensuring sustainable and long-term impact

## 4. Methodology

The Mali Solar Rural Electrification Project will adopt a participatory approach, involving local communities, government agencies, and other stakeholders in the planning, implementation, and monitoring of the project. The project will use a combination of technical, financial, and capacity-building approaches to achieve its objectives.

## 5. Expected Outcomes

The Mali Solar Rural Electrification Project is expected to achieve the following outcomes:

1. Providing clean and reliable electricity to rural communities in Mali
2. Promoting gender equality and women's empowerment
3. Enhancing the capacity of local institutions and organizations
4. Fostering public-private partnerships
5. Ensuring sustainable and long-term impact

## 6. Budget and Financing

The Mali Solar Rural Electrification Project is expected to be financed through a combination of government grants, private sector investments, and international funding. The project will require an initial investment of $50 million over five years, with additional funding available for subsequent phases.

## 7. Timeline
