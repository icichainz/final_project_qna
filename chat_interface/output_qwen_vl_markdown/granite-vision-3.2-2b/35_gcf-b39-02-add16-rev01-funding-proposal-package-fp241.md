# Funding Proposal

## Project Information

- **Project Title:** Financing Mitigation and Adaptation Projects (FMAP) in Indian MSMEs
- **Country(ies):** India
- **Accredited Entity:** Small Industries Development Bank of India
- **Date of first submission:** [2024/03/28]

## Funding Proposal Details

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is seeking funding proposals for the Financing Mitigation and Adaptation Projects (FMAP) in Indian MSMEs. The project aims to support small and medium-sized enterprises in India by providing financial assistance for projects that mitigate and adapt to climate change impacts.

## Funding Objectives

The primary objective of the FMAP is to provide financial support to eligible MSMEs in India for projects that address climate change impacts, such as:

- Climate-resilient infrastructure development
- Renewable energy adoption and integration
- Energy efficiency improvements
- Climate-smart agriculture practices

## Funding Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for funding under the FMAP, MSMEs must meet the following criteria:

1. Be incorporated in India
2. Have a minimum of 5 years of operational experience
3. Demonstrate a commitment to sustainable development and climate change mitigation
4. Provide detailed project proposals that address climate change impacts and demonstrate potential for long-term benefits

## Funding Process

The funding process involves the following steps:

1. Submission of complete and accurate project proposals by eligible MSMEs
2. Review of completed projects by a panel of experts in climate change mitigation and adaptation
3. Selection of eligible projects based on their potential for impact reduction, feasibility, and alignment with the GCF's funding objectives
4. Awarding of funding to selected projects
5. Monitoring and evaluation of project progress and outcomes

## Conclusion

The Green Climate Fund is seeking funding proposals for the Financing Mitigation and Adaptation Projects (FMAP) in Indian MSMEs. The project aims to support small and medium-sized enterprises in India by providing financial assistance for projects that mitigate and adapt to climate change impacts. Eligible MSMEs must meet specific criteria, including being incorporated in India, having a minimum of 5 years of operational experience, demonstrating a commitment to sustainable development and climate change mitigation, and providing detailed project proposals that address climate change impacts and demonstrate potential for long-term benefits. The funding process involves submission of complete and accurate project proposals by eligible MSMEs, review by a panel of experts in climate change mitigation and adaptation, selection of eligible projects based on their potential for impact reduction, feasibility, and alignment with the GCF's funding objectives, awarding of funding to selected projects, and monitoring and evaluation of project progress and outcomes.
