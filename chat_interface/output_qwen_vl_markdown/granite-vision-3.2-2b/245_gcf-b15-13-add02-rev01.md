# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum II

## Funding proposal package for FP029

### Summary

This addendum contains the following three parts:

1. **A) A funding proposal summary titled "SFC Capital Solutions" submitted by No-objectection Bars issued by Development Bank of Southern Africa.**
2. **B) A detailed description of the proposed project, including its objectives, scope, and expected outcomes.**
3. **C) A financial analysis of the proposed project, including a breakdown of costs and revenue projections.**

### Notes

- These documents are presented as submitted by the accredited entity to the national designated authority (es) or local point(s), respectively.
- Pursuant to the Comprehensive Information Disclosure Policy of the Fund, the funding proposal shall be circulated on a limited distribution basis only to Board Members and Alternate Board Members to review and provide comments prior to the final decision.
- The information contained in this addendum is based solely on the information provided by the accredited entity and does not constitute an offer or solicitation of investment.
