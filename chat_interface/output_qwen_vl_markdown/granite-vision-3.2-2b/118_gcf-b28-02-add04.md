### Funding Proposal

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is pleased to announce the funding proposal for the project titled "Coastal Resilience to Climate Change in Cuba through Ecosystem-Based Adaptation – Nr CO3147". This project aims to enhance the resilience of coastal communities in Cuba to the impacts of climate change, with a focus on ecosystem-based adaptation.

### Project Description

The project will support the implementation of a range of adaptation measures at the local level, including:

1. **Community Engagement**: Conducting community consultations and workshops to identify local priorities and needs for climate change adaptation.
2. **Infrastructure Development**: Improving coastal infrastructure, such as sea walls and dikes, to protect against flooding and erosion.
3. **Ecosystem Restoration**: Restoring natural ecosystems, such as mangroves and salt marshes, which can help absorb and store carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.
4. **Sustainable Livelihoods**: Promoting sustainable livelihoods through the development of alternative income sources, such as ecotourism and aquaculture.
5. **Capacity Building**: Providing training and capacity building programs for local communities to enhance their ability to adapt to climate change.

### Project Budget

The total budget for this project is USD 10 million over a period of five years, with the following breakdown:

- Year 1: Community engagement and infrastructure development (USD 2 million)
- Year 2: Ecosystem restoration and sustainable livelihoods (USD 3 million)
- Year 3: Capacity building and monitoring and evaluation (USD 2.5 million)
- Year 4: Implementation of adaptation measures and monitoring and evaluation (USD 1.5 million)
- Year 5: Review and reporting (USD 0.5 million)

### Project Timeline

The project is expected to be completed within five years, with the following milestones:

- Year 1: Completion of community engagement and infrastructure development
- Year 2: Completion of ecosystem restoration and sustainable livelihoods
- Year 3: Completion of capacity building and monitoring and evaluation
- Year 4: Implementation of adaptation measures and monitoring and evaluation
- Year 5: Review and reporting

### Conclusion

The Green Climate Fund is committed to supporting the implementation of this project, which will help enhance the resilience of coastal communities in Cuba to the impacts of climate change. The project's focus on ecosystem-based adaptation aligns with the GCF's mission to support countries in their efforts to mitigate and adapt to climate change.

### b) The Need for a Gender Perspective in Communities

Extensive literature and global experience has shown that women and men are often affected differently by climate change, including through norms and beliefs, power imbalances and differentiated roles in the household and public spheres, thus re-sponsies everyday situations as well as differently. Furthermore, it is also widely documented that women experience the effects of climate change differently than men, both in terms of direct impacts and indirect impacts on their livelihoods and decision-making power, which generally positions women in more vulnerable situations. It is therefore necessary to introduce a gender equity approach to project design, in order to ensure that the needs and concerns of women are taken into account throughout the life cycle of the project.

To understand the gendered differences in impacts between both men, women, girls and boys, it is important to conduct a gender analysis/assessment at an early stage of the project design process.
