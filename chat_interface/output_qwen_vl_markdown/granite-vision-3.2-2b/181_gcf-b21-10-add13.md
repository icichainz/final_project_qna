# Detailed Project / Programme Description

## Objective

The objective of this project is to assess and mitigate the impacts of climate change on water resources in Grand Comore. The study aims to identify vulnerable areas, predict future changes in water availability, and propose strategies for sustainable water management.

## Scope

The scope of this project includes:
- Analyzing historical and projected water availability data.
- Identifying key factors contributing to water scarcity and drought.
- Assessing the impacts of climate change on agriculture, industry, and human settlements.
- Developing recommendations for improving water management practices in Grand Comore.

## Methodology

The methodology involves:
- Conducting a comprehensive review of existing literature and data on water resources in Grand Comore.
- Analyzing historical and projected water availability data using appropriate statistical methods.
- Identifying key factors contributing to water scarcity and drought through field surveys, interviews with local stakeholders, and analysis of climate models.
- Assessing the impacts of climate change on agriculture, industry, and human settlements through case studies and expert consultations.
- Developing recommendations for improving water management practices in Grand Comore based on the findings of the study.

## Expected Outcomes

The expected outcomes of this project include:
- A comprehensive understanding of the current state of water resources in Grand Comore, including the factors contributing to water scarcity and drought.
- Identification of key areas for improvement in water management practices in Grand Comore.
- Recommendations for improving water management practices in Grand Comore based on the findings of the study.
- A report summarizing the methodology, findings, and recommendations of the project.

## Budget

The budget for this project is $100,000 over a period of 24 months. The budget will be used to cover the costs of field surveys, interviews with local stakeholders, analysis of climate models, case studies, expert consultations, and development of recommendations.
