# Site-Based Gender Assessment and Action Plan

## Executive Summary

This report presents a site-based gender assessment and action plan for the Department of Women, Youth and Persons with Disabilities (DMH) in South Africa. The assessment was conducted to identify gender disparities in access to financial resources, employment opportunities, and decision-making processes within the sector. The report highlights several challenges faced by women in the rural areas of South Africa and recommends strategies to address these issues.

## Introduction

The Department of Women, Youth and Persons with Disabilities (DMH) is responsible for promoting gender equality and empowering women in South Africa. However, despite efforts to improve access to financial resources, employment opportunities, and decision-making processes within the sector, women continue to face significant challenges. This report presents a site-based gender assessment and action plan to address these issues and promote gender equality in the DMH.

## Methodology

The site-based gender assessment was conducted through a combination of surveys, interviews, and focus group discussions with women in the rural areas of South Africa. The survey collected data on women's access to financial resources, employment opportunities, and decision-making processes within the sector. Interviews were conducted with women who had experienced gender discrimination or harassment in the workplace. Focus group discussions were held with women from different age groups and socioeconomic backgrounds to gather insights on their experiences and challenges.

## Findings

The site-based gender assessment revealed several gender disparities in access to financial resources, employment opportunities, and decision-making processes within the DMH. Women in the rural areas of South Africa face significant challenges in accessing financial resources, including limited access to banking services and high interest rates on loans. Women also face barriers in employment opportunities, including discrimination based on gender and age. Additionally, women are underrepresented in decision-making processes within the sector, with few women holding leadership positions.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings of the site-based gender assessment, the following recommendations are made to address gender disparities in access to financial resources, employment opportunities, and decision-making processes within the DMH:

1. Increase access to financial resources for women by providing them with banking services and low-interest loans.
2. Implement policies and programs that promote equal pay for equal work, including measures such as flexible working hours and parental leave.
3. Provide training and education on gender equality and empowerment for women in the rural areas of South Africa.
4. Increase representation of women in decision-making processes within the sector by implementing measures such as quotas and mentorship programs.
5. Implement policies and programs that promote equal access to employment opportunities for women, including measures such as targeted recruitment efforts and job training programs.
6. Provide support and resources for women who have experienced gender discrimination or harassment in the workplace, including counseling services and legal assistance.
7. Implement policies and programs that promote equal access to decision-making processes within the sector, including measures such as transparent decision-making processes and accountability mechanisms.
8. Increase awareness of gender equality and empowerment among women and men in South Africa through public campaigns and education initiatives.
9. Implement policies and programs that promote equal access to land, energy, information, and technology for women, including measures such as targeted investments and capacity-building programs.
10. Increase awareness of gender equality and empowerment among women and men in South Africa through public campaigns and education initiatives.

## Conclusion

The site-based gender assessment revealed several gender disparities in access to financial resources, employment opportunities, and decision-making processes within the Department of Women, Youth and Persons with Disabilities (DMH) in South Africa. The recommendations presented in this report aim to address these issues and promote gender equality in the sector. By implementing policies and programs that promote equal access to financial resources, employment opportunities, and decision-making processes, as well as by increasing awareness of gender equality and empowerment among women and men in South Africa, we can work towards a more equitable society where all individuals have the opportunity to thrive.

# Site-Based Gender Assessment and Action Plan

## Introduction

The consultation process will ensure meaningful inclusive participation that promotes inclusivity, transparency, accountability and non-discrimination. In addition, this emphasis ensures that gender considerations are included in all aspects of the project, including planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation.

## Key Principles

The key principles that will underlie stakeholder engagements include:

* Respect, courtesy, openness and transparency in approach, ensuring inclusivity and sensitivity to diverse perspectives, including those related to gender considerations and cultural values.
* Inclusive and transparent consultation throughout the iterative adaptation planning process, including a focus on the private sector, sub-national governments and civil society organisations.
* Ensuring clear communication and accessible information dissemination on climate impact, risk and vulnerability information to relevant stakeholders, recognising and addressing diverse needs based on the nature of the project.
* Strengthening stakeholder engagement processes within existing broader development planning and implementation processes, including through the use of participatory methods such as focus group discussions, community consultations and public meetings.
* Continuous communication and engagement to ensure progress is monitored and evaluated for equitable value for money, considering gender-inclusive impacts.
* Continuing and active stakeholder engagement throughout the process.

## Stakeholder Engagement

Stakeholders will need to be regularly kept informed of project developments, updates, and community comments. This will enable them to provide feedback on the project and key decisions made during its implementation. More details regarding the stakeholder engagement plan can be found in Annex 7.
