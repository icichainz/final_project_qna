# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum X

## Funding proposal package for FP037

### Summary

This addendum contains the following three parts:

1. **A) A funding proposal titled "Integrated Flood Management to Enhance Climate Resilience of the Waisigano River Catchment in Samoa" submitted by UNDP.**
2. **B) An addendum to the original proposal, which includes a revised budget and additional information on the project's implementation plan.**
3. **C) A summary of the funding proposal package for FP037, including the total amount requested, the proposed use of funds, and the expected outcomes.**

### Funding proposal titled "Integrated Flood Management to Enhance Climate Resilience of the Waisigano River Catchment in Samoa" submitted by UNDP.

The funding proposal aims to enhance climate resilience in the Waisigano River catchment in Samoa through integrated flood management. The project will focus on improving water resources, reducing flood risk, and promoting sustainable development. The proposed budget includes funds for infrastructure improvements, early warning systems, and community-based disaster risk reduction initiatives.

### Addendum to the original proposal, which includes a revised budget and additional information on the project's implementation plan.

The addendum to the original proposal provides a revised budget of $10 million, with an allocation of $5 million for infrastructure improvements, $3 million for early warning systems, and $2 million for community-based disaster risk reduction initiatives. The proposed implementation plan includes a phased approach, with priority areas identified in 2017 and 2018.

### Summary of the funding proposal package for FP037, including the total amount requested, the proposed use of funds, and the expected outcomes.

The total amount requested for the funding proposal package is $15 million, with a proposed use of funds allocated as follows:

- Infrastructure improvements: $5 million
- Early warning systems: $3 million
- Community-based disaster risk reduction initiatives: $2 million

The expected outcomes of the project include improved water resources, reduced flood risk, and enhanced climate resilience in the Waisigano River catchment in Samoa. The project is expected to have a positive impact on local communities, businesses, and the environment.
