## Project Components, Outputs and Activities

The project is structured around three interrelated components, the complementarities of which tackle the drivers of deforestation and forest degradation in the targeted areas in a holistic manner.

1. **Component 1: Land-use and resources planning and strengthening of land access and security rights.**
   - Component 1 puts in place the elements necessary for the successful establishment of the fuelwood forestry and agroforestry systems required for combating deforestation and forest degradation envisaged under components 2 and 3.

2. **Component 2: Establishment of agroforestry and forestry systems for climate change mitigation.**
   - Component 2 establishes the necessary institutions, policies and mechanisms to support the implementation of the project's objectives.

3. **Component 3: Strengthening of agroforestry financing structures, business capacities and value chains**
   - Component 3 strengthens the financial resources, business capabilities and value chains for the successful implementation of the project's objectives.

## Output 1.1: Enabling actions in place

Activity 1.1: Development of participatory mapping

This mapping* will be carried out with the communities before the establishment of the agroforestry and forestry systems required for combating deforestation and forest degradation envisaged under components 2 and 3. The mapping will be used to identify the areas most at risk of deforestation and forest degradation, as well as the potential benefits of implementing the project's objectives.

1. **Deforestation Mitigation**: Implementing sustainable land use practices such as agroforestry, silvopastoralism, and community-based forest management to reduce deforestation rates.

3. **Community Engagement**: Engaging with local communities to raise awareness about the importance of forest conservation and involving them in the implementation of the project activities.
4. **Monitoring and Evaluation**: Monitoring the progress of the project and evaluating its impact on greenhouse gas emissions reductions, biodiversity conservation, and socio-economic development.

## Project Financing

The project will be financed through a combination of government grants, international loans, and private sector investments. The total project budget is USD 46,567,138, with a request for a grant of USD 29,988,852 from the Ministry of Forest Economy (MEFE).

## Project Partners

1. **Government**: The Republic of the Congo government will provide funding and support for the implementation of the project activities.

4. **Local Communities**: Local communities will be engaged in the implementation of the project activities through community-based forest management and other participatory approaches.

## Project Timeline

The project is expected to be completed over a period of 20 years, with the following milestones:

1. **Year 1-3**: Conducting a baseline assessment of greenhouse gas emissions and identifying areas for deforestation mitigation and reforestation activities.
2. **Year 4-6**: Implementing deforestation mitigation and reforestation activities in the five departments and major urban areas.
3. **Year 7-9**: Monitoring and evaluating the progress of the project and making necessary adjustments to the project activities.
4. **Year 10-20**: Completing the project activities and reporting the results to stakeholders.

The PREFORREST Congo project aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from forests in five departments in the Republic of the Congo through deforestation mitigation and reforestation activities. The project will contribute to direct carbon emissions reductions, biodiversity conservation, and socio-economic development.
