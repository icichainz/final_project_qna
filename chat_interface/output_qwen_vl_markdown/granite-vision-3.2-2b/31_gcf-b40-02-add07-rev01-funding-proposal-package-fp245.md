# Green Climate Fund Funding Proposal V3.0 | Page 5

## A

### Introduction

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is a global initiative that aims to mobilize $100 billion annually by 2020 to support climate action in developing countries. This proposal outlines the funding requirements for the GCF's Programme of Action (POA), which focuses on reducing greenhouse gas emissions and enhancing adaptation to climate change.

### Climate Impacts / Benefits

The GCF will support projects that have a direct impact on reducing greenhouse gas emissions or enhancing adaptation to climate change. These projects can be categorized into two main areas:

1. **Reducing Greenhouse Gas Emissions:**
   - Renewable energy and energy efficiency projects (e.g., solar, wind, hydroelectric power)
   - Energy storage solutions
   - Carbon capture and storage technologies
   - Sustainable land use practices that reduce deforestation and promote reforestation

2. **Enhancing Adaptation to Climate Change:**
   - Early warning systems for extreme weather events (e.g., floods, droughts, heatwaves)
   - Infrastructure projects that improve resilience to climate change impacts (e.g., sea walls, levees, green roofs)
   - Disaster risk reduction and management programs
   - Climate-smart agriculture practices

### Funding Requirements

The GCF will provide funding for the following categories:

1. **Renewable Energy:**
   - Solar photovoltaic (PV) systems
   - Wind turbines
   - Hydroelectric power plants
   - Energy storage solutions (e.g., batteries, pumped hydro storage)

2. **Energy Efficiency:**
   - Building energy efficiency projects
   - Industrial energy efficiency projects
   - Transportation energy efficiency projects

3. **Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS):**
   - CCS technologies for industrial processes
   - CCS technologies for power generation

4. **Sustainable Land Use:**
   - Reforestation and afforestation projects
   - Sustainable agriculture practices
   - Integrated land-use, water, and energy management (ILWEM) projects

5. **Disaster Risk Reduction and Management:**
   - Early warning systems for extreme weather events
   - Disaster risk reduction and management programs
   - Climate-smart infrastructure projects

6. **Climate-Smart Agriculture:**
   - Climate-resilient agricultural practices
   - Agroforestry and silvopastoral systems
   - Climate information services for farmers

### Eligibility Criteria

Projects must meet the following eligibility criteria:

1. **Location:** Projects must be located in developing countries.
2. **Impact:** Projects must have a direct impact on reducing greenhouse gas emissions or enhancing adaptation to climate change.
3. **Sustainability:** Projects must be sustainable and long-lasting.
4. **Cost:** Projects must be cost-effective and feasible.
5. **Feasibility:** Projects must be technically feasible and have a clear plan for implementation.

### Conclusion

The Green Climate Fund will provide funding for projects that have a direct impact on reducing greenhouse gas emissions or enhancing adaptation to climate change in developing countries. The GCF's Programme of Action (POA) focuses on renewable energy, energy efficiency, carbon capture and storage, sustainable land use, disaster risk reduction and management, and climate-smart agriculture. Projects must meet the eligibility criteria outlined above to be considered for funding.
