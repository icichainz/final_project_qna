# Greenpeace Fund

## III. Assessment

The request from the accredited entity that will enable it to expand the programme is prescribed on formal engagement with the NDAs, pursuant to the requirements of the GCF Policy on Restructuring and Cancellation. Through a series of engagements, the accredited entity has obtained 17 objectives in writing (NDGs) from seven of nine host countries, and 23 objectives in writing (NDGs) from all six countries to which it wishes to expand its programme. The accredited entity is consulting with the NDAs of the most countries, has received a call from the NDA of the most countries to include the accredited entity as a member of the Board and to adopt the draft decision contained in annex II.

## IV. Compliance with GCF policies

In submitting this document for Board consideration, the Secretariat confirms that all relevant requirements have been complied with. The documents required by the GCF Policy on Restructuring and Cancellation have been submitted to the NDLs of the most countries, and the NDLs have confirmed that they are in compliance with the policy.

## V. Recommendation

It is recommended that the Board approve the inclusion of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Morocco, Tajikistan, and Uzbekistan as host countries for FPDBs.
