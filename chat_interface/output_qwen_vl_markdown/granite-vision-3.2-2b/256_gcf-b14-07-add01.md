# NDA No-Objection Letter

## Annex I: NDA No-Objection Letter

### GREEN CLIMATE FUND FUNDING PROPOSAL

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is a global initiative that aims to mobilize $100 billion annually by 2020 to support climate action in developing countries. The GCF's funding proposal outlines the following key points:

1. **Project Focus**: The GCF will prioritize projects that address climate change mitigation and adaptation, with a focus on vulnerable communities and low-income countries.

2. **Eligibility Criteria**: Projects must meet specific eligibility criteria, including demonstrating a clear need for the project, aligning with the GCF's mandate, and having a viable plan for implementation and monitoring.

3. **Funding Amounts**: The GCF will provide funding in the form of grants to support projects that have been approved by national governments or other eligible organizations.

4. **Project Duration**: Projects are expected to be implemented over a period of five years, with clear milestones and reporting requirements.

5. **Monitoring and Evaluation**: The GCF will require regular monitoring and evaluation of project progress, including the use of standardized indicators and reporting frameworks.

6. **Transparency and Accountability**: The GCF will ensure transparency in its funding process, including the publication of project proposals, award decisions, and financial reports.

7. **Capacity Building**: The GCF will provide capacity building support to national governments and other eligible organizations to help them effectively implement projects that align with their mandates.

8. **Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation**: Projects funded by the GCF are expected to contribute to climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts, including reducing greenhouse gas emissions and enhancing resilience to climate-related impacts.

9. **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**: Projects funded by the GCF should align with the SDGs, which aim to achieve a more sustainable future for all people on Earth.

10. **Transparency and Accountability**: The GCF will ensure transparency and accountability in its operations, including regular reporting on project outcomes and impacts.
