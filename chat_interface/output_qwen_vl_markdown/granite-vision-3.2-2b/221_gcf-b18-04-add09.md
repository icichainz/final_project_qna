The project will involve collaboration between local communities, government agencies, and international organizations.

4. **Monitoring and Evaluation Plan (MEP):** A plan that outlines the methods and indicators to be used to monitor and evaluate the project's performance. The MEP should also include measures for ensuring transparency and accountability in the project's implementation and management.
5. **Risk Management Plan (RMP):** A plan that outlines the measures to be taken to identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with the LRMP. The RMP should also include procedures for managing emergencies and crises that may arise during the project's implementation.
6. **Environmental and Social Management Plan (ESMP):** A plan that outlines the measures to be taken to ensure that the LRMP is implemented in an environmentally sustainable and socially responsible manner. The ESMP should include measures for managing waste, water, air, and other resources, as well as procedures for engaging with local communities and stakeholders.
7. **Procurement Plan:** A plan that outlines the procedures to be followed in the procurement of goods and services required for the LRMP. The Procurement Plan should include measures for ensuring transparency, fairness, and competitiveness in the procurement process.
8. **Quality Assurance Plan:** A plan that outlines the measures to be taken to ensure the quality of the LRMP's outputs and deliverables. The Quality Assurance Plan should include procedures for monitoring and evaluating the project's performance against its objectives and standards.
9. **Monitoring and Evaluation Plan:** A plan that outlines the procedures to be followed in the monitoring and evaluation of the LRMP's progress and impact. The Monitoring and Evaluation Plan should include measures for collecting, analyzing, and reporting data on the project's activities and outcomes.
10. **Risk Management Plan:** A plan that outlines the measures to be taken to identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with the LRMP. The Risk Management Plan should include procedures for monitoring and evaluating the project's risk profile and implementing risk management strategies.
