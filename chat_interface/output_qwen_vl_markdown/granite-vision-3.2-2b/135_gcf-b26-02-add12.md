Moroccan laws are primarily based on French civil law and Islamic Sharia law (UNICEF, 2011) and men and women are equal before the law (Art. 6 of the Constitution).

## Female Participation in the Labour Force

Women's low level of labor force participation is attributed to high levels of unemployment, informality and low levels of education compared to men's (ILO, ILOSTAT, 2014). This is particularly striking in rural areas where almost 100% of women are in informal employment. Women are also more likely to be unemployed than men due to their role as primary caregivers for children and the lack of access to education (UNICEF, 2015). Despite progress towards gender equality, women still face numerous barriers to economic participation including discriminatory laws, cultural norms and social expectations.

## Introduction

This document provides an overview of low-carbon sectoral roadmaps for high impact industries and knowledge sharing. It discusses the importance of these roadmaps in promoting gender equality, addressing economic development, and mitigating climate change. ## Low-Carbon Sectoral Roadmaps for High Impact Industries

Low-carbon sectoral roadmaps are strategies that aim to reduce greenhouse gas emissions while promoting economic growth and job creation. These roadmaps focus on high impact industries, such as renewable energy, sustainable transportation, and green technologies. By investing in these sectors, countries can create new opportunities for employment and innovation while reducing their carbon footprint.

## Knowledge Sharing

Knowledge sharing is a critical component of low-carbon sectoral roadmaps. It involves the exchange of information, best practices, and lessons learned among stakeholders, including governments, businesses, and civil society organizations. By sharing knowledge, countries can learn from each other's experiences and develop more effective strategies for achieving their low-carbon goals.

## Gender Equality

Gender equality is a key component of low-carbon sectoral roadmaps. Women are often disproportionately affected by climate change and economic inequality, making them vulnerable to the negative impacts of low-carbon policies. By promoting gender equality, countries can ensure that women have equal access to opportunities for employment, education, and decision-making.

## The EBRD's Gender Policy and Action Plan

The EBRD's Gender Policy and Action Plan is a strategic document that outlines the organization's commitment to promoting gender equality in its operations and activities. The plan includes specific targets for increasing the representation of women in leadership positions, improving access to education and training, and addressing the unique challenges faced by women in low-carbon sectors.

## Conclusion

Low-carbon sectoral roadmaps for high impact industries and knowledge sharing are critical components of achieving sustainable development goals. By promoting gender equality, addressing economic development, and mitigating climate change, these roadmaps can help create a more equitable and prosperous future for all. The EBRD's Gender Policy and Action Plan is an important tool for achieving these goals and ensuring that women have equal access to opportunities for employment, education, and decision-making.
