The objectives of the GAP are:

1. To promote gender equality and women's empowerment in all FAO programs and activities.
2. To ensure that gender considerations are integrated into all FAO policies, strategies, and programs.
3. To enhance the capacity of FAO institutions to address gender issues and promote gender equality.
4. To monitor and evaluate the progress of gender equality initiatives in FAO programs and activities.

## Strategies

The GAP outlines several strategies to achieve its objectives:

1. Strengthening Gender Equality Policies and Programs: The GAP aims to strengthen gender equality policies and programs by integrating gender considerations into all FAO policies, strategies, and programs. 2. Enhancing Gender Capacity: The GAP aims to enhance the capacity of FAO institutions to address gender issues by providing training and capacity building programs for women and girls in all FAO programs and activities. This includes training on gender equality, women's empowerment, and sustainable development.
3. Monitoring and Evaluation: The GAP aims to monitor and evaluate the progress of gender equality initiatives in FAO programs and activities by developing indicators and monitoring systems that track gender-related data. This includes tracking data on gender disparities in food security, nutrition, and sustainable development.
4. Strengthening Gender Dialogue: The GAP aims to strengthen gender dialogue by promoting gender equality discussions and debates at all levels of FAO institutions. This includes providing platforms for women and girls to participate in decision-making processes and share their perspectives on gender issues.

## Implementation

The implementation of the GAP will involve several steps, including:

1. Developing a Gender Equality Policy Framework: The GAP aims to develop a Gender Equality Policy Framework that outlines the policies, strategies, and programs that promote gender equality in all FAO programs and activities. This framework will be developed by involving women and girls in the development process.
2. Strengthening Gender Capacity through Training and Capacity Building Programs: The GAP aims to strengthen the capacity of FAO institutions to address gender issues by providing training and capacity building programs for women and girls in all FAO programs and activities. This includes training on gender equality, women's empowerment, and sustainable development.
3. Monitoring and Evaluation through Gender-Sensitive Indicators: The GAP aims to monitor and evaluate the progress of gender equality initiatives in FAO programs and activities by developing indicators and monitoring systems that track gender-related data. 4. Strengthening Gender Dialogue through Gender Equality Discussions: The GAP aims to strengthen gender dialogue by promoting gender equality discussions and debates at all levels of FAO institutions. This includes providing platforms for women and girls to participate in decision-making processes and share their perspectives on gender issues.
