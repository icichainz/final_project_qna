### Introduction

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is a global initiative that aims to mobilize $100 billion annually by 2020 to support climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts in developing countries. This proposal outlines the funding requirements for a specific project, which will focus on improving access to markets and establishing Climate Risk Assessment (CRA) markets in Africa.

### Project Overview

The proposed project aims to enhance the capacity of smallholder farmers in Africa to understand and manage climate risks associated with their agricultural activities. The project will focus on developing and implementing a CRA framework that will enable farmers to assess and mitigate climate risks, thereby improving their resilience and productivity.

### Project Objectives

1. To enhance the capacity of smallholder farmers in Africa to understand and manage climate risks associated with their agricultural activities.
2. To develop and implement a CRA framework that will enable farmers to assess and mitigate climate risks, thereby improving their resilience and productivity.
3. To promote the use of climate-smart agricultural practices among smallholder farmers in Africa.
4. To enhance the capacity of local governments and other stakeholders to support the implementation of the CRA framework.
5. To disseminate knowledge and best practices on climate risk management among smallholder farmers in Africa.

### Project Activities

1. Conduct a needs assessment to identify the specific climate risks faced by smallholder farmers in Africa.
2. Develop a CRA framework that will enable farmers to assess and mitigate climate risks, thereby improving their resilience and productivity.
3. Train local governments and other stakeholders on the use of the CRA framework.
4. Promote the use of climate-smart agricultural practices among smallholder farmers in Africa.
5. Disseminate knowledge and best practices on climate risk management among smallholder farmers in Africa.

### Project Budget

The proposed budget for this project is $10 million over five years, with the following breakdown:

* Year 1: Needs assessment, CRA framework development, and training of local governments and other stakeholders.
* Year 2: Implementation of the CRA framework among smallholder farmers in Africa.
* Year 3: Promotion of climate-smart agricultural practices among smallholder farmers in Africa.
* Year 4: Dissemination of knowledge and best practices on climate risk management among smallholder farmers in Africa.
* Year 5: Evaluation of the project's impact and preparation for the next funding cycle.
