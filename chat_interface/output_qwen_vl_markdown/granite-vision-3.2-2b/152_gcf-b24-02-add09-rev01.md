# Climate Bonds for Forests: Scaling up Private Sector Financing for REDD+

## Introduction

The funding proposal titled "Climate Bonds for Forests: Scaling up Private Sector Financing for REDD+" submitted by the International Finance Corporation (IFC) has been withdrawn from consideration at the twenty-first session of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC).

## Background

The proposal aimed to mobilize additional financing from private sector investors for REDD+ projects, which are designed to reduce greenhouse gas emissions in developing countries. The project would have involved issuing climate bonds that would be used to finance REDD+ activities, such as reforestation and afforestation projects.
