# Ministry of Economy Letter

## Executive Director

## The Green Climate Fund

## Songolo Business District

## Republic of Korea

---

10 August 2017

Mr Howard Barnsey
Executive Director
The Green Climate Fund
Songolo Business District
Republic of Korea

Dear Executive Director,

I am writing to inform you that the Ministry of Economy has reviewed the funding proposal submitted by the Korea Development Bank on behalf of the Biomass Energy Programme in the South Pacific. The proposal is for a project in the Republic of Fiji, which was approved by the GCF in June 2017.

The Ministry of Economy has reviewed the proposal and has determined that it does not meet the criteria set out in the funding proposal submitted by the Korea Development Bank. The proposal did not include a detailed description of how the project would generate biogas, nor did it demonstrate compliance with relevant national laws and regulations.

The Ministry of Economy is therefore unable to approve the funding proposal as submitted by the Korea Development Bank for this project in the Republic of Fiji. The Ministry will continue to work with the Korea Development Bank to ensure that future funding proposals meet the criteria set out in the funding proposal submitted by the GCF.

We apologize for any inconvenience caused by this decision and appreciate your understanding. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Sincerely,

Makoto Kasumoto
Permanent Secretary for Economy
National Designated Authority for the Green Climate Fund
