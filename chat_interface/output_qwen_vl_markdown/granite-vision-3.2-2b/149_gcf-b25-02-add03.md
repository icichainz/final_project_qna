# Funding Proposal

## Project Programme title: Increased climate resilience of rural households and communities through the rehabilitation of production landscapes in selected locations of the Republic of Cuba

## Country: Cuba

## Accredited Entity: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations

## Date of current submission: 2023-09-15

## Version number: V.107

## Funding Proposal

The Green Climate Fund (GCF) is pleased to announce that it has approved a funding proposal for the project titled "Increased climate resilience of rural households and communities through the rehabilitation of production landscapes in selected locations of the Republic of Cuba." The project aims to enhance the resilience of rural households and communities to the impacts of climate change by improving their access to natural resources, such as water and fertile soil, and by promoting sustainable agricultural practices.

The project will be implemented in selected locations of the Republic of Cuba, with a focus on areas that are most vulnerable to the impacts of climate change. The project will involve the rehabilitation of production landscapes, including the restoration of degraded soils and the promotion of agroforestry practices. The project will also include the provision of training and capacity building for rural households and communities on sustainable agricultural practices and climate change adaptation strategies.

The funding proposal is based on a detailed budget, which includes the following components:

1. Project management and coordination: $500,000
2. Rehabilitation of production landscapes: $300,000
3. Training and capacity building for rural households and communities: $200,000
4. Monitoring and evaluation: $100,000
5. Contingency fund: $50,000

The total funding request is $1,250,000. The project will be implemented over a period of five years, with the following milestones:

1. Year 1: Project planning and preparation
2. Year 2: Rehabilitation of production landscapes
3. Year 3: Training and capacity building for rural households and communities
4. Year 4: Monitoring and evaluation
5. Year 5: Final report and dissemination of results

The project is expected to have a significant impact on the resilience of rural households and communities in the Republic of Cuba, as well as on the sustainability of agricultural production in the region. The project will also contribute to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) related to climate change adaptation and mitigation.

The Green Climate Fund is committed to ensuring that the funding proposal is implemented in accordance with the principles of transparency, accountability, and good governance. The project will be subject to regular monitoring and evaluation to ensure that it is achieving its objectives and delivering value for money.
