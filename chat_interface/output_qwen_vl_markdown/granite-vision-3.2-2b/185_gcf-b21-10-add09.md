## 1. Introduction

This document provides a detailed description of the Green Climate Fund (GCF) project, including its objectives, scope, methodology, budget, financial management, risk management, stakeholder engagement, monitoring and evaluation, and conclusion. The GCF is a global fund that supports projects in developing countries to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and enhance climate resilience.

## 2. Project Objectives and Scope

The primary objective of the GCF project is to support projects in developing countries that are committed to reducing their greenhouse gas emissions and enhancing their climate resilience. The scope of the project includes a range of activities, including:

- Supporting projects that reduce greenhouse gas emissions through renewable energy, energy efficiency, and sustainable land use practices.
- Enhancing climate resilience through early warning systems, disaster risk reduction measures, and climate-smart infrastructure development.
- Promoting sustainable agriculture and forestry practices that reduce greenhouse gas emissions and enhance carbon sequestration.
- Supporting projects that promote sustainable water management and reduce water scarcity.

## 3. Financial Viability

The financial viability of the GCF project was determined through a comparison of its expected returns to its costs. The expected returns were based on a range of scenarios, including best-case, base-case, and worst-case scenarios. The costs were estimated based on the project's technical requirements, market conditions, and other factors.

# Detailed Project / Programme Description

## Objective
The objective of this project is to provide a detailed description of the Green Climate Fund (GCF) funded proposal for the Tonga Village Network Upgrade Project. The project aims to improve energy efficiency and reduce greenhouse gas emissions in the village by upgrading the existing power grid infrastructure.

## Scope
The scope of this project includes the following:
- Upgrading the existing power grid infrastructure
- Implementing energy efficiency measures
- Reducing greenhouse gas emissions
- Improving access to electricity for households and businesses

## Project Timeline
The project is expected to be completed within 12 months from the start date. The following milestones are outlined in the project timeline:
- Month 1-3: Planning and preparation
- Month 4-6: Design and procurement of equipment
- Month 7-9: Installation of equipment
- Month 10-12: Testing and commissioning

## Budget
The total budget for this project is $5,000,000. The budget includes the following components:
- Equipment and machinery costs
- Labor costs
- Project management and administration costs
- Contingency funds

## Conclusion
This project aims to improve energy efficiency and reduce greenhouse gas emissions in Tonga Village Network by upgrading the existing power grid infrastructure. The project is expected to be completed within 12 months, with a total budget of $5,000,000.

## Background

The project TREP is a renewable energy investment programme that aims to generate clean and sustainable energy for Tonga. The project is in line with the objectives of the GCF and the funding proposal submitted by ADB.

## Project Description

The project TREP involves the development of renewable energy projects in Tonga, including solar power plants, wind turbines, and hydroelectric power plants. The project is expected to generate clean and sustainable energy for Tonga, reduce greenhouse gas emissions, and promote economic growth and job creation.

## Funding Proposal

The funding proposal submitted by ADB includes a total of $10 million in funding over five years. The funding will be used to support the development of renewable energy projects in Tonga, including the construction of solar power plants, wind turbines, and hydroelectric power plants. The funding will also be used to support the training and capacity building of local communities in the use of renewable energy technologies.

We appreciate the opportunity to submit a funding proposal for the GCF by ADB regarding Tonga Renewable Energy Project (TREP) /Pacific Islands Renewable Energy Investment Programme. We believe that the project is in line with the objectives of the GCF and the funding proposal submitted by ADB.
