# Funding Proposal

## Project Information

- **Project Title:** Technical Assistance (TAI) Facility for the Global Subnational Climate Action Fund (GSCAF)
- **Project Programme title:** GSCAF Global Equity, Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation
- **Project Code:** GSCAF-2023-01
- **Project Description:** Technical Assistance to support the implementation of climate change adaptation and mitigation projects in developing countries.
- **Project Duration:** 2 years (2023-2025)
- **Project Budget:** USD 1,500,000
- **Project Partners:** International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN), World Bank, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), and other relevant organizations.

## Project Objectives

1. To provide technical assistance to developing countries in implementing climate change adaptation and mitigation projects.
2. To support the development of national climate action plans and strategies.
3. To enhance the capacity of local governments, communities, and other stakeholders in addressing climate change impacts.
4. To promote sustainable development and reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

## Project Activities

1. **Capacity Building:** Training workshops for government officials, policymakers, and community leaders on climate change adaptation and mitigation strategies.
2. **Project Design and Implementation:** Assistance in designing and implementing climate change adaptation and mitigation projects, including project management, monitoring, and evaluation.
3. **Financing:** Support for accessing financing from international financial institutions and other sources to support the implementation of climate change adaptation and mitigation projects.
4. **Monitoring and Evaluation:** Assistance in developing and implementing monitoring and evaluation systems to track progress towards climate change adaptation and mitigation goals.
5. **Advocacy:** Advocacy for increased funding and policy support for climate change adaptation and mitigation projects.

## Project Partners

1. International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN)
2. World Bank
3. United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)
4. Other relevant organizations, including national governments, local communities, and civil society organizations.

## Accredited Entity

The project is accredited by the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN).

Additionally, country ownership could help to reduce corruption and promote transparency in the management of natural resources.

# Step 5b Establish meaningful gender performance indicators for the Project

## WHAT ARE GENDER INDICATORS?

Indicators play a crucial role in the gender responsive process throughout the policy cycle. Quantitative and qualitative indicators must enable ongoing monitoring and evaluation of whether the Project's gender equality objectives are being met and assessing the gender effect of Project activities. If objectives are not being met, it could be essential to re-assess the Project strategy and make amendments to ensure improvements. Gender indicators usually express how discrimination is manifested in a particular situation or from gender equality and are usually expressed as quantitative data such as percentages, ratios and generally as measures of gender inequalities. Gender blind indicators are those that do not allow the evaluator to uncover gender differences and gender relations in a society because they are not collected or cannot be collected in a sex-disaggregated way. For example, "community participation" is a gender blind indicator but if collected separately for men and for women, can become a gender indicators.
