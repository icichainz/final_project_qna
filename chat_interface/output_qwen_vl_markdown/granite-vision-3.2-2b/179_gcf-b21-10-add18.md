# Consideration of funding proposals - Addendum XVIII

## Funding proposal package for FP096

### Summary

This addendum contains the following three parts:

1. A funding proposal summarized as "DRC Green Mini-Grid Program" submitted by the African Development Bank.
2. No objection letter issued by the national designated authorities or focal points; and
3. Environmental and social report(s) disclosure.

These documents are presented as submitted by the accredited entity and the national designated authority(ies). The African Development Bank is being circulated on a limited confidentiality basis to ensure that all parties involved have access to the most up-to-date information regarding the funding proposal.

# GREEN CLIMATE FUND FUNDING PROPOSAL

## I. Supporting Documents for Funding Proposal

### A. NDA No objection Letter

- **Purpose:** To confirm that the project is eligible for funding under the Green Climate Fund.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### B. Feasibility Study (Market Study)

- **Purpose:** To assess the market potential and demand for the proposed project.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### C. Integrated Financial Model that provides sensitivity analysis of critical elements (xls format, if applicable)

- **Purpose:** To provide a detailed financial model that includes sensitivity analysis of critical elements.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### D. Confirmatory letter or letter of commitment for co-financing commitment (if applicable)

- **Purpose:** To confirm the availability and willingness of a co-financing partner to provide additional funding.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### E. Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) or Environmental and Social Management Plan (ESMP)

- **Purpose:** To assess the potential environmental and social impacts of the proposed project.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### F. Appraisal Report or Due Diligence Report with recommendations (if applicable)

- **Purpose:** To provide an assessment of the project's potential risks and benefits, and make recommendations for further action.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### G. Map indicating the location of the project/programme

- **Purpose:** To provide a visual representation of the project's location.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### H. Timetable for project/programme implementation

- **Purpose:** To provide a detailed schedule for the implementation of the project.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### I. Evaluation Report or Due Diligence Report with recommendations (if applicable)

- **Purpose:** To provide an assessment of the project's potential risks and benefits, and make recommendations for further action.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.

### J. Appendix: Additional supporting documents

- **Purpose:** To include any additional supporting documents that are relevant to the funding proposal.
- **Submission:** Required by the applicant to be submitted along with the proposal.
