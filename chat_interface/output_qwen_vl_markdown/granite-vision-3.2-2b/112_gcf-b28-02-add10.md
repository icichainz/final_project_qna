## Funding Proposal

The Global Renewables Finance Facility (GRMF) is seeking funding proposals for the Sustainable Renewables Risk Mitigation Initiative (SRMI). The SRMI aims to support the development and deployment of renewable energy projects in Africa, Central America, the Republic of Congo (DRC), Kenya, Mali, Namibia and Uzbekistan.

The funding proposal should address the following key areas:

1. **Project Overview**: Provide a brief overview of the proposed project, including its location, scale, and expected impact on renewable energy development in the target countries.

2. **Risk Mitigation Measures**: Describe the risk mitigation measures that will be implemented to ensure the sustainability and success of the project. This may include financial, technical, operational, and environmental risk management strategies.

3. **Financial Projections**: Provide detailed financial projections for the proposed project, including revenue streams, cost estimates, and expected returns on investment.

4. **Implementation Plan**: Outline a comprehensive implementation plan, including timelines, milestones, and responsible parties for each phase of the project.

5. **Monitoring and Evaluation**: Describe the monitoring and evaluation strategies that will be used to track progress and ensure accountability throughout the project lifecycle.

6. **Sustainability and Social Impact**: Address how the proposed project aligns with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and contributes to sustainable development in the target countries.

7. **Stakeholder Engagement**: Describe the stakeholder engagement strategies that will be used to ensure the involvement and support of local communities, governments, and other relevant parties throughout the project lifecycle.

8. **Legal and Regulatory Framework**: Provide an overview of the legal and regulatory framework in place for renewable energy development in the target countries, including any relevant policies, laws, or agreements that may impact the project.

9. **Environmental Impact Assessment**: Describe the environmental impact assessment process that will be used to evaluate the potential environmental effects of the proposed project and ensure compliance with environmental regulations.

10. **Risk Management Plan**: Outline a comprehensive risk management plan, including strategies for identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks associated with the proposed project.

The funding proposal should be submitted in accordance with the GRMF's guidelines and requirements, which can be found on the GRMF website. The submission deadline is [insert date], and proposals will be reviewed based on their alignment with the GRMF's objectives and priorities.

9. **Climate Change Mitigation in Forests**: Projects that protect and restore forests, which act as natural carbon sinks and provide other ecosystem services.

10. **Compliance with Funding Rules**: A statement confirming that the applicant will comply with all applicable funding rules, regulations, and guidelines.
