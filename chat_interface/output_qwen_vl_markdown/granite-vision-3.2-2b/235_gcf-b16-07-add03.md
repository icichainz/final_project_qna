# Detailed Project / Programme Description

## Object / Programme Description

The object of this project is to improve energy security and climate resilience in a specific region. This will be achieved through various initiatives such as improving distribution grid, developing new tariff policies, and implementing gender-sensitive practices.

## Project Scope

The project will focus on the following areas:

1. Improving Distribution Grid: The project aims to improve the distribution grid by reducing losses and increasing efficiency. This will be achieved through various measures such as upgrading infrastructure, improving maintenance, and implementing smart technologies.

2. Developing New Tariff Policies: The project will develop new tariff policies that are fair and transparent. These policies will take into account the needs of both households and businesses, and will aim to promote energy efficiency and reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

3. Implementing Gender-Sensitive Practices: The project will implement gender-sensitive practices in all aspects of the project. This includes ensuring that women are involved in decision-making processes, providing equal access to resources and opportunities, and addressing gender-based discrimination.

## Project Timeline

The project is expected to be completed within a period of three years. The following milestones will be achieved:

1. Completion of the distribution grid improvement by the end of year one.
2. Development of new tariff policies by the end of year two.
3. Implementation of gender-sensitive practices throughout the project.

## Project Budget

The budget for this project is USD 50 million. The budget will be allocated as follows:

1. Distribution grid improvement: USD 20 million
2. Development of new tariff policies: USD 15 million
3. Implementation of gender-sensitive practices: USD 10 million
4. Project management and administration: USD 5 million

## Project Partners

The project will be implemented by a consortium of international organizations, including the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), the International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA), and the World Bank. The partners will work together to ensure that the project is implemented in a transparent, accountable, and sustainable manner.

## Stakeholders

The stakeholders involved in this project include:

* Government agencies responsible for energy security and climate resilience
* Private sector companies involved in the distribution of electricity
* Households and businesses affected by power cuts and energy poverty
* Local communities and organizations involved in community development and social inclusion

* Social risks related to the impact on households and communities affected by power cuts and energy poverty
* Environmental risks related to the impact on local ecosystems and biodiversity.
